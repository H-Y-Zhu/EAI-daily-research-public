ContactFlow: A video action conditioning that transfers across embodiments

Sami Azirar, Enrico Pallotta, Jan Nogga, Jürgen Gall, Sven Behnke, Hermann Blum
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给机器人世界模型喂了个“接触流”大补汤。作者觉得现有的视频生成模型要么太关注机器人长啥样（关节角度），要么太关注手影（mask），都不如直接盯着“手和物体碰哪儿了”来得实在。Contact Flow 把 3D 接触点投影到图像，强行让人类手和机械臂共用一套“语言”。虽然听起来很性感，能跨实体迁移，但仔细一看，这玩意儿极度依赖上游的 3D 重建和 SAM3 分割，一旦深度不准或者遮挡严重，接触点就飘了。而且推理成本不低，还要靠 VLM 来当裁判，真·“想象一下再动手”，适合实验室里慢慢磨，离实时控制还差着几光年。不过，能把人类和机器人数据混训，确实解决了世界模型“偏科”的老毛病，为跨实体操作提供了新思路。
This paper feeds robot world models a 'Contact Flow' potent tonic. The authors believe existing video generation models either focus too much on robot appearance (joint angles) or too much on hand shadows (masks), none as practical as directly focusing on "where the hand and object make contact". Contact Flow projects 3D contact points onto images, forcing human hands and robotic arms to share a common "language". Although it sounds appealing and enables cross-embodiment transfer, upon closer inspection, this approach relies extremely heavily on upstream 3D reconstruction and SAM3 segmentation; once depth is inaccurate or occlusion is severe, the contact points become unstable. Moreover, the inference cost is not low, and it relies on VLMs to act as referees, truly "imagine before acting", suitable for gradual refinement in the lab, still several light-years away from real-time control. However, mixing human and robot data for training indeed solves the chronic "bias" problem of world models, providing new ideas for cross-embodiment manipulation.

研究动机Motivation

具身智能的核心难点在于让机器人理解物理交互，而现有的视频世界模型往往缺乏物理 faithfulness [13, 14]。更致命的是，动作条件编码通常绑定特定实体（embodiment-specific）。比如基于关节角度的表示 [10, 15] 无法迁移到不同形态的机器人，而基于 Actor mask 或轮廓的方法 [16, 17] 又过分强调执行器的形状而非交互本身。这就导致模型学的是“怎么动”，而不是“怎么碰”。作者指出物理操纵由接触决定，无论手还是夹爪，只要接触点轨迹一致，效果就该一样。因此，需要一种解耦外观和运动学的接触几何信号，实现人类演示与机器人执行的零样本迁移。现有方法如 Kinema4D [35] 虽尝试跨实体，但仍关注机器人几何，未完全解耦。
The core challenge in embodied intelligence lies in enabling robots to understand physical interaction, while existing video world models often lack physical faithfulness [13, 14]. More critically, action condition encoding is typically embodiment-specific. For instance, representations based on joint angles [10, 15] cannot transfer to robots of different morphologies, while methods based on Actor masks or contours [16, 17] overemphasize the actuator's shape rather than the interaction itself. This results in the model learning "how to move" rather than "how to touch". The authors point out that physical manipulation is determined by contact; regardless of whether it is a hand or a gripper, if the contact point trajectory is consistent, the outcome should be identical. Therefore, a contact geometry signal that decouples appearance and kinematics is required to achieve zero-shot transfer from human demonstrations to robot execution. Existing methods such as Kinema4D [35] attempt cross-embodiment transfer but still focus on robot geometry and do not fully decouple.

方法Method

整体流程是“提议 - 想象 - 验证 - 执行”。核心创新是 Contact Flow 编码：将 actor 与目标物体间的 3D 接触点轨迹投影到图像空间，形成 7 通道稀疏控制帧（位置、位移、置信度）。训练时，使用 Wan [11] 作为骨干，通过 ControlNet [20] 或 VACE [12] 注入该信号，混合人类（HaMeR, HACO）和机器人（URDF, 点云）数据。数据预处理是关键，人类数据需估计手部姿态和接触区域，机器人数据则利用 URDF 和深度图直接计算接触点。推理阶段，从末端轨迹合成接触流，输入世界模型生成未来视频，再由 VLM 评估是否成功，仅通过验证的轨迹才执行。这种设计使得模型只关注物体侧的交互界面，完全忽略执行器外观，实现了跨实体泛化。此外，模型在推理时仅依赖末端轨迹和场景模型，无需特定机器人状态，进一步增强了泛化能力。
The overall workflow is "Propose - Imagine - Verify - Execute". The core innovation is Contact Flow encoding: projecting 3D contact point trajectories between the actor and target object into image space to form 7-channel sparse control frames (position, displacement, confidence). During training, Wan [11] is used as the backbone, with this signal injected via ControlNet [20] or VACE [12], mixing human (HaMeR, HACO) and robot (URDF, point cloud) data. Data preprocessing is critical; human data requires estimation of hand pose and contact regions, while robot data utilizes URDF and depth maps to directly calculate contact points. In the inference phase, contact flow is synthesized from end-effector trajectories, input into the world model to generate future videos, then evaluated by a VLM for success; only validated trajectories are executed. This design ensures the model focuses solely on the interaction interface on the object side, completely ignoring actuator appearance, thereby achieving cross-embodiment generalization. Furthermore, during inference, the model relies only on end-effector trajectories and scene models, without requiring specific robot states, further enhancing generalization capabilities.

实验Experiments

在 DROID 数据集和真实 Franka Panda 机械臂上测试。对比基线包括 Kinema4D [35]、CTRL-World 和 TesserAct [23]。指标涵盖 DreamSim、FID、FVD 等。结果显示，Contact Flow 在跨数据集评估中表现优异，特别是在未见过的物体和场景上，DreamSim 和 PSNR 均优于基线。在真实任务验证中，结合 VLM 的验证步骤使得 10 个 unseen 场景中 8 个成功，而直接部署 VLA 策略则全失败。消融实验表明混合人类和机器人数据训练效果最佳。不过，依赖外部深度估计和 SAM3 分割也带来了误差传播风险，且推理速度尚未达到实时。实验还验证了不同骨干网络（5B/14B）和控制机制（ControlNet/VACE）的稳定性，证明了 Contact Flow 的通用性。
Testing was conducted on the DROID dataset and a real Franka Panda robot arm. Comparison baselines include Kinema4D [35], CTRL-World, and TesserAct [23]. Metrics cover DreamSim, FID, FVD, etc. Results indicate that Contact Flow performs excellently in cross-dataset evaluation, particularly on unseen objects and scenes, with both DreamSim and PSNR outperforming the baselines. In real task verification, the verification step combined with VLM resulted in 8 out of 10 unseen scenes succeeding, whereas directly deploying VLA strategies failed completely. Ablation studies demonstrate that training with mixed human and robot data yields the best performance. However, reliance on external depth estimation and SAM3 segmentation introduces risks of error propagation, and inference speed has not yet achieved real-time capabilities. Experiments also verified the stability of different backbone networks (5B/14B) and control mechanisms (ControlNet/VACE), proving the universality of Contact Flow.
