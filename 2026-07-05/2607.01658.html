Teaching Vision-Language-Action Models What to See and Where to Look

Yuguang Yang, Canyu Chen, Zhewen Tan, Yizhi Wang, Zichao Feng, Chunyang Liu
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给 VLA 自动驾驶模型上了一堂“视觉课”：别光会背交通规则，得知道眼睛往哪看。现有 VLA 模型太依赖文本问答（VQA）和思维链（CoT），导致注意力图像撒胡椒面一样分散，缺乏空间 grounding。DriveTeach-VLA 搞了个双模型架构，一个负责“看”（TGP-Prompter），一个负责“开”（TGP-Planner）。用 DVD 蒸馏视觉先验，用 2D-TGP 指引空间位置。结果确实牛，NAVSIM 上 PDMS 90.4 领跑。但双模型推理慢了点，虽然比 AutoVLA 快，但总归是多了个环节。不过，把“看”和“开”解耦，确实解决了注意力分散的痛点，算是给 VLA 灌了点“视觉营养剂”。虽然论文声称双模型增加了推理时间，但通过减少 CoT token 数量，整体效率依然优于竞品。不过，依赖 Grounding DINO 检测质量是个隐患，一旦检测漏掉关键车辆，蒸馏的视觉先验就可能带偏模型。
This paper delivers a 'visual lesson' to VLA autonomous driving models: do not just memorize traffic rules, but know where to look. Existing VLA models rely too heavily on text question answering (VQA) and chain-of-thought (CoT), causing attention maps to scatter like pepper, lacking spatial grounding. DriveTeach-VLA employs a dual-model architecture, one responsible for 'seeing' (TGP-Prompter), one for 'driving' (TGP-Planner). Using DVD to distill visual priors, and 2D-TGP to guide spatial positions. The results are indeed impressive, leading with PDMS 90.4 on NAVSIM. But dual-model inference is a bit slower; although faster than AutoVLA, it still adds an extra step. However, decoupling 'seeing' and 'driving' does solve the pain point of scattered attention, essentially giving VLA some 'visual nutrients'. Although the paper claims dual models increase inference time, by reducing CoT token count, overall efficiency remains superior to competitors. However, relying on Grounding DINO detection quality is a risk; if key vehicles are missed during detection, the distilled visual priors could mislead the model.

研究动机Motivation

自动驾驶 VLA 模型正从模块化转向端到端，但训练数据多基于文本（VQA/CoT）。这导致模型学会“命名”物体而非理解空间关系。传统模块化设计虽可解释，但误差传播严重。而现有 VLA 虽统一，却因文本监督导致语义知识丰富但空间依赖缺失。这就像让司机背交规却不让看路，容易出事故。现有工作如 AutoVLA [55]、RecogDrive [29] 虽引入 RL，但视觉感知仍依赖文本监督，注意力图分散（Fig 1a）。缺乏空间依赖导致轨迹预测不可靠。本文旨在解决感知与规划间的鸿沟，让模型明确“看什么”和“看哪里”。
Autonomous driving VLA models are shifting from modular to end-to-end, but training data is mostly text-based (VQA/CoT). This leads models to learn 'naming' objects rather than understanding spatial relationships. Traditional modular designs are interpretable but suffer from severe error propagation. While existing VLA models are unified, text supervision leads to rich semantic knowledge but lacks spatial dependency. This is like making a driver memorize traffic rules without allowing them to look at the road, leading to accidents. Existing works like AutoVLA [55], RecogDrive [29] introduce RL, but visual perception still relies on text supervision, with scattered attention maps (Fig 1a). Lack of spatial dependency leads to unreliable trajectory prediction. This paper aims to bridge the gap between perception and planning, enabling the model to clearly define 'what to look at' and 'where to look'.

方法Method

整体流程分三步：DVD 预训练 -> TGP 引导 SFT -> TGP 引导 GRPO。核心是双模型：TGP-Prompter 生成 2D-TGP 空间引导，TGP-Planner 基于此预测 BEV 轨迹。
1. DVD 预训练：用 Grounding DINO [39] 检测关键物体，生成 bbox-augmented 图像。Teacher ViT 看增强图，Student ViT 看原图，通过 block-wise alignment 对齐特征，注入视觉先验。同时 SFT 训练解码器预测 2D-TGP。蒸馏中使用 EMA 更新 Teacher 权重，Block-wise 对齐损失采用 Smooth-L1 以增强鲁棒性。
2. TGP 引导：将专家轨迹从 BEV 投影到 2D 图像平面（针孔模型），作为文本坐标条件输入 Planner。保留 yaw 角以便反投影验证。
3. 训练：Planner 用 CoT 数据 SFT，再用 GRPO [13] 优化。训练时用 GT 2D-TGP（teacher forcing），推理时用 Prompter 预测的 2D-TGP。
The overall process is divided into three steps: DVD pre-training -> TGP-guided SFT -> TGP-guided GRPO.
The core is a dual model: TGP-Prompter generates 2D-TGP spatial guidance, TGP-Planner predicts BEV trajectories based on this.
1. DVD Pre-training: Use Grounding DINO [39] to detect key objects, generating bbox-augmented images.
Teacher ViT views augmented images, Student ViT views original images, aligning features via block-wise alignment to inject visual priors.
Simultaneously, SFT trains the decoder to predict 2D-TGP.
EMA is used to update Teacher weights during distillation, and Block-wise alignment loss uses Smooth-L1 to enhance robustness.
2. TGP Guidance: Project expert trajectories from BEV to 2D image plane (pinhole model), inputting as text coordinate conditions to the Planner.
Retain yaw angle for inverse projection verification.
3. Training: Planner uses CoT data for SFT, then optimizes with GRPO [13].
During training, GT 2D-TGP is used (teacher forcing); during inference, 2D-TGP predicted by Prompter is used.

实验Experiments

在 NAVSIM (Navtest) 和 nuScenes 上测试。NAVSIM 用 PDMS 指标，DriveTeach-VLA 达 90.4，优于 AutoVLA [55] (89.1)。nuScenes L2 0.30，碰撞率 0.12。消融显示 DVD 和 TGP 均有效（Table 5）。注意力质量提升（AM 分数，Table 6）。推理延迟虽因双模型增加，但比 AutoVLA 快（Table 10）。2D-TGP 预测误差对性能影响小（Fig 5），鲁棒性尚可。超参数 λTGP 设为 0.1 时效果最佳。Block 大小 2x4 优于 Patch 级对齐。Train-test 差距仅 0.4 PDMS，证明 Prompter 生成的 2D-TGP 足够可靠。
Tested on NAVSIM (Navtest) and nuScenes. NAVSIM uses PDMS metric, DriveTeach-VLA reaches 90.4, superior to AutoVLA [55] (89.1). nuScenes L2 0.30, collision rate 0.12. Ablation shows both DVD and TGP are effective (Table 5). Attention quality improves (AM score, Table 6). Inference latency increases due to dual models, but is faster than AutoVLA (Table 10). 2D-TGP prediction error has little impact on performance (Fig 5), robustness is acceptable. Hyperparameter λTGP set to 0.1 yields best results. Block size 2x4 outperforms Patch-level alignment. Train-test gap is only 0.4 PDMS, proving 2D-TGP generated by Prompter is sufficiently reliable.
