Influence of Radial Basis Activation Functions on Intelligent Controller for Robotic Manipulators

Kimmo Paldanius, Gabriel Da Silva Lima, Wallace Moreira Bessa
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给控制理论圈做了一次“核函数选美”。作者没搞端到端大模型，而是把反馈线性化和 RBF 神经网络凑一块，然后问：到底哪个激活函数更好用？在控制领域，稳定性是底线，性能是上限。他们证明了只要 Lyapunov 分析过关，啥核都能稳，但稳不代表好用。IMQ 核在平滑轨迹上表现惊艳，误差砍半，但在阶跃信号上反而因为“太激进”导致超调。这就像选车，IMQ 是跑车，直线快但过弯抖；高斯核是家用车，稳但不够快。结论很实在：激活函数不是实现细节，是设计参数。不过，1-DOF 实验略显单薄，多自由度呢？数字孪生毕竟不是真机，摩擦模型够准吗？总之，这是一篇给控制工程师的“避坑指南”，而非颠覆性创新，胜在严谨的变量隔离。
This paper conducts a 'kernel function beauty contest' for the control theory community. The authors did not pursue end-to-end large models, but instead combined feedback linearization and RBF neural networks, then asked: which activation function actually works better? In the control field, stability is the bottom line, and performance is the upper limit. They proved that as long as Lyapunov analysis passes, any kernel can be stable, but stability does not imply usability. The IMQ kernel performed amazingly on smooth trajectories, halving the error, but on step signals, it instead caused overshoot due to being 'too aggressive'. This is like choosing a car: IMQ is a sports car, fast in a straight line but shaky in turns; Gaussian kernel is a family car, stable but not fast enough. The conclusion is very practical: activation functions are not implementation details, but design parameters. However, the 1-DOF experiment is slightly thin, what about multi-degree-of-freedom? After all, digital twins are not real machines, is the friction model accurate enough? In short, this is a 'pitfall avoidance guide' for control engineers, rather than disruptive innovation, winning on rigorous variable isolation.

研究动机Motivation

机器人轨迹跟踪常依赖基于模型的非线性控制（如反馈线性化），但实际中参数不确定、摩擦和未建模动态会让精度大打折扣（Ccari et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2025）。自适应控制虽能处理结构化不确定性，但对未建模动态往往力不从心。神经网络因其通用逼近能力被引入作为扰动估计器（Brunke et al., 2022; Zeng et al., 2025）。然而，现有研究大多默认使用高斯核（Gaussian kernel），将其视为实现细节而非设计变量（Niu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2026）。这种“默认主义”忽略了核函数形状对逼近特性及自适应权值演化的影响。若核函数选择不当，可能导致稳态精度低或瞬态响应过激。本文旨在填补这一空白，系统探究不同 RBF 核（高斯、拉普拉斯、逆多二次）在相同控制结构下的表现，明确其作为结构参数对闭环性能的具体影响，为智能控制器设计提供实证依据。
Robot trajectory tracking often relies on model-based nonlinear control (such as feedback linearization), but in practice, parameter uncertainty, friction, and unmodeled dynamics significantly reduce accuracy (Ccari et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2025). Although adaptive control can handle structured uncertainty, it is often powerless against unmodeled dynamics. Neural networks are introduced as disturbance estimators due to their universal approximation capability (Brunke et al., 2022; Zeng et al., 2025). However, existing research mostly defaults to using Gaussian kernels, treating them as implementation details rather than design variables (Niu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2026). This 'defaultism' ignores the impact of kernel function shape on approximation characteristics and adaptive weight evolution. If the kernel function is chosen improperly, it may lead to low steady-state accuracy or overly aggressive transient response. This paper aims to fill this gap, systematically investigating the performance of different RBF kernels (Gaussian, Laplace, Inverse Multiquadric) under the same control structure, clarifying their specific impact on closed-loop performance as structural parameters, and providing empirical evidence for intelligent controller design.

方法Method

整体架构采用“模型 + 学习”的混合策略。首先，基于机器人动力学方程，利用反馈线性化抵消已知非线性项，将系统转化为积分器链形式。其次，引入 RBF 神经网络作为在线扰动估计器，补偿摩擦、参数误差及未建模动态。核心创新在于自适应律的设计：采用基于 Lyapunov 的更新规则，确保闭环信号有界且跟踪误差收敛至紧集。为防止权值发散，引入了投影算法将权值约束在凸区域内。关键变量是激活函数，论文对比了三种核：高斯（局部、快速衰减）、拉普拉斯（局部、尖峰）和逆多二次（全局支持）。实验设置中，控制器增益、学习率、神经元数量及投影边界均固定，仅改变激活函数，以隔离变量影响。这种设计剥离了其他干扰，纯粹考察核函数特性对逼近误差及控制平滑度的影响，体现了控制理论中对结构参数的精细化考量。
The overall architecture adopts a 'model + learning' hybrid strategy. First, based on robot dynamic equations, feedback linearization is used to cancel known nonlinear terms, transforming the system into an integrator chain form. Second, an RBF neural network is introduced as an online disturbance estimator to compensate for friction, parameter errors, and unmodeled dynamics. The core innovation lies in the design of the adaptive law: adopting a Lyapunov-based update rule to ensure closed-loop signals are bounded and tracking error converges to a compact set. To prevent weight divergence, a projection algorithm is introduced to constrain weights within a convex region. The key variable is the activation function; the paper compares three kernels: Gaussian (local, fast decay), Laplace (local, spike), and Inverse Multiquadric (global support). In the experimental setup, controller gains, learning rate, number of neurons, and projection boundaries are fixed, only changing the activation function to isolate variable influence. This design strips away other interferences, purely examining the impact of kernel function characteristics on approximation error and control smoothness, reflecting the refined consideration of structural parameters in control theory.

实验Experiments

实验在 Quanser QArm 的数字孪生环境中进行，单自由度机械臂，采样频率 500Hz。对比了正弦、方波、三角波三种参考轨迹。基线为无神经网络的反馈线性化，实验组分别使用高斯、拉普拉斯、IMQ 核。评价指标包括 RMS 跟踪误差、绝对误差积分、控制量 RMS，阶跃响应还包含超调量和调节时间。结果显示：IMQ 核在平滑轨迹上优势明显，正弦误差降低 51.9%，三角波降低 49.0%，且控制量增加不足 3%。但在方波测试中，IMQ 和拉普拉斯核因局部适应性强导致超调增加和调节时间延长。这表明全局支持核适合稳态精度，局部核在突变指令下可能引发瞬态过激。消融分析证实，激活函数选择直接决定了逼近流形和权值演化，而非单纯稳定性问题。
Experiments were conducted in the Quanser QArm digital twin environment, a single-degree-of-freedom manipulator, with a sampling frequency of 500Hz. Three reference trajectories were compared: sine, square wave, and triangle wave. The baseline was feedback linearization without neural networks, while experimental groups used Gaussian, Laplace, and IMQ kernels respectively. Evaluation metrics include RMS tracking error, integral of absolute error, control RMS, and step response also includes overshoot and settling time. Results show: IMQ kernel has obvious advantages on smooth trajectories, sine error reduced by 51.9%, triangle wave reduced by 49.0%, and control input increase is less than 3%. However, in square wave tests, IMQ and Laplace kernels caused increased overshoot and extended settling time due to strong local adaptability. This indicates that global support kernels are suitable for steady-state accuracy, while local kernels may trigger transient aggression under sudden command changes. Ablation analysis confirms that activation function selection directly determines the approximation manifold and weight evolution, rather than being merely a stability issue.
