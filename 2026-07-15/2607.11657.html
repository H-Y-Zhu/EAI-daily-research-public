Trajectory Planning and Certification for 3-DOF Robot Manipulators Using Real Quantifier Elimination Based on Comprehensive Gröbner Systems

Yu Nakai, Akira Terui, Masahiko Mikawa
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文把代数几何的硬核武器搬到了机器人轨迹规划上。作者用 Comprehensive Gröbner Systems (CGS) 和 Real Quantifier Elimination (QE) 来解决 3-DOF 机械臂的逆运动学问题。优点是数学上严谨，能证明解的存在性，不像数值方法那样容易陷入局部最优或无解。缺点是计算量巨大，毕竟 CGS 计算本身就很耗时，虽然他们优化了参数化系统，但实时性恐怕堪忧。这更像是一个“理论验证”而非“工程落地”的方案，适合对安全性要求极高的场景，而不是需要毫秒级响应的工业现场。相比之前的 6-DOF 工作，这里牺牲自由度换取计算可行性，依然无法摆脱符号计算的沉重包袱。
This paper applies the powerful tools of algebraic geometry to robot trajectory planning. The authors use Comprehensive Gröbner Systems (CGS) and Real Quantifier Elimination (QE) to solve the inverse kinematics problem of a 3-DOF manipulator. The advantage is mathematical rigor, capable of proving the existence of solutions, unlike numerical methods which are prone to falling into local optima or having no solution. The disadvantage is huge computational load, after all CGS calculation itself is time-consuming, although they optimized the parameterized system, real-time performance is probably worrying. This is more like a "theoretical verification" rather than an "engineering implementation" scheme, suitable for scenarios with extremely high safety requirements, rather than industrial sites needing millisecond-level response. Compared to previous 6-DOF work, here degrees of freedom are sacrificed for computational feasibility, still unable to shake off the heavy burden of symbolic computation.

研究动机Motivation

现有数值方法（如 Jacobian 逆解）虽然快，但无法保证全局可行性，容易在奇异点附近失效 [6]。传统的 Gröbner 基方法虽然能求全局解，但每次轨迹点都要重新计算，效率太低。作者团队之前做过 6-DOF 的研究 [8, 9]，但这里聚焦 3-DOF 以提升效率。主要动机是解决轨迹规划中“解是否存在”的严格证明问题，特别是对于复杂轨迹（如三次样条），需要确保整条路径上逆运动学都有解，而不仅仅是离散点。之前的工作仅支持线段，本文扩展到了样条，并计算可行参数范围，填补了精确规划中的空白。
Existing numerical methods (such as Jacobian inverse solution) are fast, but cannot guarantee global feasibility and are prone to failure near singular points [6]. Traditional Gröbner basis methods can find global solutions, but need to recalculate for each trajectory point, efficiency is too low. The author team previously conducted 6-DOF research [8, 9], but here focuses on 3-DOF to improve efficiency. The main motivation is to solve the strict proof problem of "whether a solution exists" in trajectory planning, especially for complex trajectories (such as cubic splines), requiring ensuring inverse kinematics has solutions along the entire path, not just discrete points. Previous work only supported line segments, this paper extends to splines, and calculates feasible parameter ranges, filling the gap in exact planning.

方法Method

整体流程分为建模、CGS 预处理和 QE 认证三步。首先，基于 D-H 参数将 myCobot 的逆运动学转化为多项式方程组，通过引入 c=sin/cos 变量将三角函数转为多项式 [2]。核心创新在于使用 Comprehensive Gröbner Systems (CGS) 处理参数化系统（末端坐标为参数），预先计算 CGS，避免了对轨迹上每个点重复计算 Gröbner 基。接着，利用 CGS-QE 方法，通过计算剩余类环上的乘法映射特征多项式，将“解存在”的命题转化为关于轨迹参数 r 的一阶逻辑公式。最后，通过符号计算消除量词，得到轨迹参数 r 的可行区间。算法在 Risa/Asir 系统中实现，支持线段和三次自然样条轨迹，并给出了具体的参数范围计算算法（Alg 1）。
The overall process is divided into three steps: modeling, CGS preprocessing, and QE certification. First, based on D-H parameters, convert myCobot's inverse kinematics into a system of polynomial equations, by introducing c=sin/cos variables to convert trigonometric functions into polynomials [2]. The core innovation lies in using Comprehensive Gröbner Systems (CGS) to handle parameterized systems (end-effector coordinates as parameters), precomputing CGS, avoiding repeated calculation of Gröbner bases for each point on the trajectory. Next, utilize the CGS-QE method, by computing the characteristic polynomial of the multiplication map on the residue class ring, transform the proposition "solution exists" into a first-order logic formula regarding trajectory parameter r. Finally, eliminate quantifiers through symbolic computation to obtain the feasible interval for trajectory parameter r. The algorithm is implemented in the Risa/Asir system, supports line segments and cubic natural spline trajectories, and provides a specific parameter range calculation algorithm (Alg 1).
