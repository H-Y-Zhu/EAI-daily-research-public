CofactVLA: Deconfounding Vision-Language-Action Models via Counterfactual Intervention

Yan Zhang, Yinan Wu, Haoran Duan, Jungong Han
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给 VLA 模型治“眼病”——视觉盖过语言指令。清华团队搞了个 CofactVLA，用反事实干预把视觉干扰项给剔除了。方法挺硬核，正交投影加协方差削减，听起来就很数学。实验数据确实漂亮，OOD 场景下提升巨大，但代价是推理时多了个分支，算力可能吃不消。而且，如果基座模型本身就不懂指令，光靠干预也救不了。不过，把因果推断硬塞进 VLA 里，这思路确实比单纯堆数据要性感，至少治标又治本。
This paper treats the "eye disease" of VLA models—vision overriding language instructions. The Tsinghua team developed CofactVLA, using counterfactual intervention to eliminate visual distractors. The method is quite rigorous, involving orthogonal projection plus covariance reduction, sounding very mathematical. The experimental data is indeed impressive, with huge improvements in OOD scenarios, but the cost is an extra branch during inference, which might strain computational resources. Moreover, if the base model itself does not understand instructions, intervention alone cannot save it. However, incorporating causal inference into VLA is indeed a more appealing approach than simply stacking data, at least treating both symptoms and root causes.

研究动机Motivation

VLA 模型在机器人控制上进步很大，但有个致命弱点：视觉覆盖语言（Vision-Override）。因为训练数据里视觉流太密，语言指令太稀疏，模型容易走捷径，盯着显眼的物体看，忘了用户让干啥。现有方法要么靠数据增强 [10, 11]，要么靠推理时的引导 [13]，但前者难扩展，后者容易把动作推离分布，导致不安全。这篇论文指出这是因果混淆问题，需要从根本上解耦视觉和语言的纠缠表示，而不是事后修补。
VLA models have made great progress in robot control, but have a fatal weakness: Vision-Override. Because visual streams are too dense and language instructions too sparse in training data, models tend to take shortcuts, staring at conspicuous objects and forgetting what the user wants them to do. Existing methods rely either on data augmentation [10, 11] or inference-time guidance [13], but the former is hard to scale, and the latter easily pushes actions away from the distribution, leading to unsafe outcomes. This paper identifies this as a causal confounding problem, requiring fundamental decoupling of the entangled representations of vision and language, rather than post-hoc patching.

方法Method

整体框架叫 Dual-path Deconfounding Graph (DDG)。核心思想是构造一个语言掩码的反事实分支。前向传播时，同时跑真实分支（有图有文）和反事实分支（只有图）。
模块 1：OPG（动作级）。在连续流匹配中，把真实分支的速度场正交投影到反事实分支上，减去视觉偏差，保留纯语言意图。这比简单的标量减法更安全，防止动作分布偏移。
模块 2：CCR（特征级）。计算真实和反事实特征协方差差，惩罚正特征值空间，抑制视觉捷径，保留因果语义。这直接在注意力机制层面清洗特征。
这样在特征层和动作层双重干预，切断虚假因果路径。通过几何投影和数学惩罚，确保动作严格遵循语言指令，而非视觉捷径。
The overall framework is called Dual-path Deconfounding Graph (DDG). The core idea is to construct a counterfactual branch with a language mask. During forward propagation, both the real branch (with image and text) and the counterfactual branch (image only) are run simultaneously.
Module 1: OPG (Action-level). In continuous flow matching, the velocity field of the real branch is orthogonally projected onto the counterfactual branch, subtracting visual bias and retaining pure language intent. This is safer than simple scalar subtraction, preventing action distribution shift.
Module 2: CCR (Feature-level). Calculate the covariance difference between real and counterfactual features, penalize the positive eigenvalue space, suppress visual shortcuts, and retain causal semantics. This cleans features directly at the attention mechanism level.
Thus, dual intervention at the feature and action levels cuts off spurious causal paths. Through geometric projection and mathematical penalties, it ensures actions strictly follow language instructions, rather than visual shortcuts.

实验Experiments

仿真用了 LIBERO 和 LIBERO-Plus，真实世界用了 AgileX PiPer 机械臂。基线对比了 π0 [34], π0.5 [17], OpenVLA [14] 等。指标是成功率。结果上，LIBERO 平均 98.5%，SOTA。OOD 场景下，真实机器人成功率提升 52.3%（从 23.5% 到 75.8%）。消融实验证明 OPG 和 CCR 都有用，且正交投影比标量减法更好。特别是在复杂视觉干扰下，模型表现更稳健。
Simulation used LIBERO and LIBERO-Plus, real-world experiments used AgileX PiPer robotic arm. Baselines included comparisons with π0 [34], π0.5 [17], OpenVLA [14], etc. The metric is success rate. In results, LIBERO average 98.5%, SOTA. In OOD scenarios, real robot success rate improved by 52.3% (from 23.5% to 75.8%). Ablation experiments prove OPG and CCR are both useful, and orthogonal projection is better than scalar subtraction. Especially under complex visual interference, the model performance is more robust.
