Bimanual Manipulation Within an 8 GB Budget: Zero-Copy Sensing and Quantized ACT on an Entry-Level Jetson

Ekansh Singh, Eva Samuel, Alessandra Reneau, Ryan Schmeelk, Yashvi Gandhi
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文试图把双臂操作塞进 8GB Jetson，标题党嫌疑不小。实际上内存根本没爆，瓶颈是 CPU 调度。Zero-copy 没救内存，只救了 CPU 核心利用率。ACT 吊打 Diffusion Policy 纯属训练预算差异，别急着定论架构优劣。INT8 量化 Transformer 层全军覆没，TensorRT 工具链对混合架构支持堪忧。任务选得巧（软豆子袋），容错率高，INT8 误差大也不影响成功率，换刚性物体未必这么乐观。此外，引擎构建时的内存峰值远超推理时，部署需预留 Swap，这点容易被忽视。
This paper attempts to cram dual-arm manipulation into an 8GB Jetson, raising considerable suspicion of clickbait. In reality, memory did not actually overflow; the bottleneck was CPU scheduling. Zero-copy did not save memory, only saved CPU core utilization. ACT decisively outperforming Diffusion Policy is purely due to training budget differences; do not rush to conclude architectural superiority. INT8 quantization of Transformer layers resulted in total failure; TensorRT toolchain support for hybrid architectures is worrying. The task was chosen cleverly (soft bean bag), with high fault tolerance; large INT8 errors do not affect success rate, switching to rigid objects may not be this optimistic. Additionally, memory peaks during engine construction far exceed those during inference; deployment requires reserving Swap, a point easily overlooked.

研究动机Motivation

现有双臂操作研究（如 ALOHA [3]、LeRobot [4]）虽降低了硬件成本，但策略训练与部署仍依赖工作站级 GPU，边缘部署成本未明。ACT [1] 与 Diffusion Policy [2] 在嵌入式端的收敛成本与推理开销缺乏系统评估。本文针对 NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super（8GB 统一内存），旨在验证低成本硬件能否承载完整感知 - 决策 - 控制闭环，解决计算资源与边缘部署的矛盾。特别是针对多相机感知带来的内存与带宽压力，以及量化对 Transformer 架构的兼容性，现有工作未给出明确边界。
Existing dual-arm manipulation research (e.g., ALOHA [3], LeRobot [4]) has reduced hardware costs, but policy training and deployment still rely on workstation-level GPUs, and edge deployment costs remain unclear. ACT [1] and Diffusion Policy [2] lack systematic evaluation of convergence costs and inference overhead on embedded endpoints. This paper targets the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super (8GB unified memory), aiming to verify whether low-cost hardware can carry a complete perception-decision-control closed loop, resolving the contradiction between computing resources and edge deployment. Particularly regarding memory and bandwidth pressure brought by multi-camera perception, and quantization compatibility with Transformer architectures, existing work has not provided clear boundaries.

方法Method

系统基于 SO-101 双臂与三目 USB 相机，运行于 Jetson Orin Nano Super。感知模块创新在于 GStreamer 零拷贝管道，利用 NVIDIA NVMM 缓冲区使三路视频流直接驻留 GPU 内存，避免 Host-Device 复制开销，从而释放 CPU 算力。策略训练阶段，在 RTX 3070 上使用相同演示数据分别训练 ACT 与 Diffusion Policy，前者 100k 步，后者 200k 步，以评估不同架构在有限预算下的收敛性。推理优化阶段，将选定策略导出 ONNX，构建 TensorRT 引擎。针对 INT8 量化，使用熵校准器进行后训练量化，并详细记录了 Transformer 层与 CNN 层的接受情况。控制回路保持 10Hz，通过动作分块（Action Chunking）配置调整推理频率需求。此外，引擎构建过程被单独评估，发现其内存峰值远高于推理时，对 8GB 设备构成潜在威胁。
The system is based on the SO-101 dual-arm and three USB cameras, running on Jetson Orin Nano Super. The innovation in the perception module lies in the GStreamer zero-copy pipeline, utilizing NVIDIA NVMM buffers to allow three video streams to reside directly in GPU memory, avoiding Host-Device copy overhead, thereby releasing CPU computing power. During the policy training phase, ACT and Diffusion Policy were trained separately using the same demonstration data on an RTX 3070, the former for 100k steps and the latter for 200k steps, to evaluate convergence of different architectures under limited budgets. During the inference optimization phase, the selected policy was exported to ONNX, and a TensorRT engine was built. For INT8 quantization, an entropy calibrator was used for post-training quantization, and the acceptance of Transformer layers and CNN layers was recorded in detail. The control loop maintains 10Hz, adjusting inference frequency requirements through Action Chunking configuration. Additionally, the engine construction process was evaluated separately, finding its memory peak far exceeds that during inference, posing a potential threat to 8GB devices.

实验Experiments

实验对比传统与零拷贝感知，发现内存未受限，但 CPU 峰值占用显著下降。ACT 在 100k 步达到 95% 成功率，Diffusion Policy 在 200k 步仍失败。量化测试显示 INT8 推理延迟降至 12.65ms，加速 9 倍，但文件体积仅减 0.9%。关键发现是 TensorRT 校准器拒绝所有 145 个 Transformer 层，仅量化 ResNet18 骨干。数值偏差增大 40 倍但任务成功率持平，表明软物体任务对精度不敏感。INT4 因工具链不支持未尝试。引擎构建时 Swap 峰值达 2.9GB，提示部署需预留资源。
Experiments compared traditional and zero-copy perception, finding memory was not limited, but CPU peak usage dropped significantly. ACT reached 95% success rate at 100k steps, while Diffusion Policy still failed at 200k steps. Quantization tests showed INT8 inference latency dropped to 12.65ms, accelerated 9 times, but file size reduced only by 0.9%. The key finding is that the TensorRT calibrator rejected all 145 Transformer layers, quantizing only the ResNet18 backbone. Numerical deviation increased 40 times but task success rate remained flat, indicating soft object tasks are insensitive to precision. INT4 was not attempted due to lack of toolchain support. Swap peak during engine construction reached 2.9GB, indicating deployment requires reserving resources.
