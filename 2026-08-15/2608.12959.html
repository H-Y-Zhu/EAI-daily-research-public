The Objective Is the Bottleneck: Latent World Models Encode What Their Planners Cannot Use

Joyjeet Singh
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文像个冷静的法医，解剖了一个看似完美的具身智能模型，发现长程规划失败并非因为心脏（预测器）衰竭，而是大脑（规划器）用错了地图。作者 Joyjeet Singh 在复现 LeWorldModel 时发现，世界模型预测器在 75 步外依然有效，规划器却只敢用 25 步。最讽刺的是，模型编码里藏着完美的位置信息（R² 0.9922），但规划器用的平方欧氏距离目标函数在长距离下饱和甚至反转，导致规划器像个瞎子。更狠的是，仅替换目标函数就能把长程成功率从 26% 拉到 98%，还没动模型权重。这不仅是复现，更是给业界泼了盆冷水：别光盯着预测 Loss 选模型，规划几何结构才是命门。当然，局限也明显，只在 TwoRoom 这种简单环境验证，且未解释为何表征会组织成那样，但道理够硬，直指 Latent World Models 的核心痛点。
This paper acts as a calm forensic pathologist, dissecting a seemingly perfect embodied intelligence model, discovering that long-horizon planning failure is not due to heart (predictor) failure, but the brain (planner) using the wrong map. Author Joyjeet Singh found when reproducing LeWorldModel that the world model predictor remains effective beyond 75 steps, while the planner only dares to use 25 steps. Most ironically, perfect position information (R² 0.9922) is hidden in the model encoding, but the squared Euclidean distance objective function used by the planner saturates or even reverses at long distances, causing the planner to act like a blind person. Even more harshly, simply replacing the objective function can pull the long-horizon success rate from 26% to 98%, without touching model weights. This is not just reproduction, but pouring a basin of cold water on the industry: do not just stare at prediction Loss to select models, planning geometry structure is the critical vulnerability. Of course, limitations are obvious, only verified in simple environments like TwoRoom, and did not explain why representation organizes that way, but the argument is solid enough, pointing directly to the core pain point of Latent World Models.

研究动机Motivation

现有研究默认 Latent World Models 的预测精度直接决定规划能力，一旦长程规划失败，通常归咎于预测器 horizon 不够或容量不足。但 Singh 发现，在 LeWorldModel 复现中，预测器表现与规划能力存在解离，预测最准的 checkpoint 反而长程规划最差。引用 Li, Wang and Liu (2026) 的观点，潜在空间的欧氏距离并非可达性的好代理。本文动机在于揭示这种“预测 - 规划”解离的机制：不是模型学坏了，是评估指标（Objective）失效了。如果不解决这个问题，盲目追求预测 Loss 可能会选到规划能力差的模型。此外，现有复现工作（Singh, 2026）报告了现象但未解释机制，本文填补了这一空白，证明了病理属于方法而非实现，这对选择世界模型有重要指导意义。
Existing research assumes Latent World Models' prediction accuracy directly determines planning capability; once long-horizon planning fails, it is usually blamed on predictor horizon being insufficient or capacity being inadequate. But Singh found that in LeWorldModel reproduction, there is a decoupling between predictor performance and planning capability, where the most accurate predictor checkpoint actually has the worst long-horizon planning. Citing Li, Wang and Liu (2026)'s viewpoint, Euclidean distance in latent space is not a good proxy for reachability. This paper's motivation lies in revealing the mechanism of this "prediction-planning" decoupling: it is not that the model learned poorly, but the evaluation metric (Objective) failed. If this problem is not solved, blindly pursuing prediction Loss might select models with poor planning capability. Additionally, existing reproduction work (Singh, 2026) reported the phenomenon but did not explain the mechanism; this paper fills this gap, proving the pathology belongs to the method rather than implementation, which has important guiding significance for selecting world models.

方法Method

整体流程基于 LeWorldModel 的复现，使用 ViT-Tiny 编码器和预测器。规划器采用 CEM 搜索，原目标是最小化想象状态与目标嵌入的平方欧氏距离。诊断阶段，作者冻结编码器，通过真实位置对嵌入进行 Ridge 探针，证明位置信息完好无损。核心创新在于“目标函数修复”：1. 解码位置成本：用线性探针从嵌入恢复位置，计算位置距离。2. 学习到的时间距离成本：训练 MLP 预测两帧间的步数差，完全无位置监督。训练对来自同一 episode 的真实帧，监督信号为帧间隔。关键发现是，学习到的成本必须在规划器评分的分布（想象嵌入）上训练，否则在预测器漂移时失效。修复过程无需重训模型，仅替换规划目标。此外，论文还对比了不同训练配置对几何信息编码的影响，指出历史长度和动作维度可能影响有效维度，但这只是相关性而非因果。实验表明，在预测器漂移严重时，线性成本比 MLP 更鲁棒。
The overall process is based on LeWorldModel reproduction, using ViT-Tiny encoder and predictor. The planner adopts CEM search, with the original objective being minimizing the squared Euclidean distance between the imagined state and target embedding. In the diagnosis phase, the author freezes the encoder and performs a Ridge probe on embeddings via real positions, proving position information is intact. The core innovation lies in "Objective function repair": 1. Decoded position cost: use a linear probe to recover position from embedding and calculate position distance. 2. Learned time distance cost: train MLP to predict step difference between two frames, with completely no position supervision. Training is on real frames from the same episode, with supervision signal being frame interval. The key finding is that the learned cost must be trained on the planner scoring distribution (imagined embeddings), otherwise it fails when the predictor drifts. The repair process requires no retraining of the model, only replacing the planning objective. Additionally, the paper also compared the impact of different training configurations on geometric information encoding, pointing out that history length and action dimension might affect effective dimension, but this is correlation rather than causation. Experiments show that when predictor drift is severe, linear cost is more robust than MLP.

实验Experiments

实验在 TwoRoom 环境进行，使用四个 checkpoint 和作者发布的权重。基线是原 CEM 目标。评测指标为不同 offset（25 vs 100）下的成功率。结果显示，原目标在 offset 100 时成功率仅 26%，修复后（时间距离成本）提升至 98%，且与 horizon 无关。消融表明，预测距离最准的“解码位置”成本（r=0.9897）不如“时间距离”成本（r=0.819）规划效果好，因为后者能感知墙壁阻隔（Reachability）。在作者原始权重上，线性成本优于学习成本，因为预测器漂移导致 MLP 外推失败，验证了训练分布匹配的重要性。实验还发现，修复后的规划预算需求大幅降低，证明了目标函数才是瓶颈。
Experiments were conducted in the TwoRoom environment, using four checkpoints and author-released weights. The baseline is the original CEM objective. Evaluation metrics are success rate under different offsets (25 vs 100). Results show that the original objective had a success rate of only 26% at offset 100, which improved to 98% after repair (time distance cost), and was independent of horizon. Ablation shows that the "decoded position" cost (r=0.9897), which is most accurate at predicting distance, is not as good as the "time distance" cost (r=0.819) for planning effect, because the latter can perceive wall blocking (Reachability). On author original weights, linear cost is better than learned cost, because predictor drift causes MLP extrapolation failure, verifying the importance of training distribution match. Experiments also found that repaired planning budget demand was significantly reduced, proving the objective function is the bottleneck.
