Wave-Based Bilateral Teleoperation between Nonlinear Manipulators with Direct Contact Force Feedback

G. Q. Bao Tran, Takanori Miyoshi, Ho Duc Tho
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文试图在非线性机械臂的双边遥操作里搞点“直接力反馈”的狠活，毕竟传统波变换（WT）为了稳，往往牺牲了透明度，只传协调力不传真力。作者搞了个 LMI 来量化从动端的“缺性”（passivity shortage），然后用 USP 通信律来补窟窿。理论推导挺漂亮，Lyapunov-Krasovskii 函数玩得飞起，但实验全是仿真，2-DOF 平面机械臂，没上真机。这就好比在纸上证明了永动机可行，但没造出来。不过对于非线性多自由度系统，这确实是个不错的理论补丁，至少比那些只能处理单自由度的方法强点。延迟鲁棒性倒是保住了，但透明度优化全靠离线调参，在线适应性存疑。此外，LMI 求解依赖状态有界假设，实际物理系统中如何保证这个假设始终成立也是个问题，毕竟机械臂关节不是无限自由的。
This paper attempts to implement some 'direct force feedback' bold maneuvers in bilateral teleoperation of nonlinear manipulators, after all, traditional Wave Transformation (WT) often sacrifices transparency for stability, transmitting only coordinated force instead of true force. The authors developed an LMI to quantify the slave end's 'passivity shortage' (passivity shortage), and then used a USP communication law to compensate for the deficiency. The theoretical derivation is quite elegant, employing Lyapunov-Krasovskii functions extensively, but experiments are all simulations, 2-DOF planar manipulators, no real hardware. This is like proving perpetual motion feasible on paper, but not building it. However, for nonlinear multi-degree-of-freedom systems, this is indeed a good theoretical patch, at least better than methods that can only handle single degrees of freedom. Delay robustness is preserved, but transparency optimization relies entirely on offline tuning, online adaptability is questionable. Additionally, LMI solution depends on the bounded state assumption, how to guarantee this assumption always holds in actual physical systems is also a problem, after all, manipulator joints are not infinitely free.

研究动机Motivation

双边遥操作的核心矛盾是延迟下的稳定性与透明度的权衡。传统散射变换 [2], [3] 和波变换 [4] 虽然稳，但为了保稳定，往往不敢传真实的环境接触力，只能传协调力，导致力觉反馈失真 [12]。虽然已有工作 [13] 提出用严格被动（USP）通道补偿，但大多基于线性单自由度模型。实际机器人动力学是非线性的、多自由度的（Euler-Lagrange），直接力反馈会让从动端非被动，导致闭环失稳。现有方法 [14] 虽引入冗余散射权重，但也局限于线性模型。因此，需要一种能处理非线性多自由度动力学，同时保证延迟鲁棒性和高保真力觉反馈的框架。这不仅是理论挑战，更是提升远程操作沉浸感的关键，否则操作员就像隔着一层厚玻璃摸东西。
The core contradiction of bilateral teleoperation is the trade-off between stability and transparency under delay. Traditional scattering transformation [2], [3] and wave transformation [4] are stable, but to ensure stability, they often dare not transmit real environmental contact force, only coordinated force, leading to haptic feedback distortion [12]. Although existing work [13] proposes using strictly passive (USP) channels for compensation, most are based on linear single-degree-of-freedom models. Actual robot dynamics are nonlinear, multi-degree-of-freedom (Euler-Lagrange), direct force feedback makes the slave end non-passive, leading to closed-loop instability. Existing methods [14] although introduce redundant scattering weights, are also limited to linear models. Therefore, a framework is needed that can handle nonlinear multi-degree-of-freedom dynamics while ensuring delay robustness and high-fidelity haptic feedback. This is not only a theoretical challenge but also key to improving remote operation immersion, otherwise the operator feels like touching things through a thick layer of glass.

方法Method

整体流程是：建模 -> 缺性分析 -> 通信律设计 -> 参数优化。首先，将主从机械臂建模为 Euler-Lagrange 系统，假设从动端状态有界。接着，通过构造二次存储函数，利用 LMI 条件量化从动子系统在直接力反馈下的“缺性”水平（alpha）。然后，设计向量值的多自由度 USP 通信律，引入额外被动性（gamma_r < -alpha）来抵消缺性，确保闭环稳定。最后，通过离线优化通信参数（阻抗 b 和增益 gamma_l），最小化位置/力跟踪误差，提升透明度。关键创新在于将标量 USP 扩展到向量多自由度，并用 LMI 处理非线性动态的稳定性证明。通信通道采用波变量变换，通过延迟线连接主从，利用反射系数调节能量流。
The overall process is: Modeling -> Passivity analysis -> Communication law design -> Parameter optimization. First, model the master-slave manipulators as Euler-Lagrange systems, assuming the slave end state is bounded. Next, by constructing a quadratic storage function, use LMI conditions to quantify the passivity level (alpha) of the slave subsystem under direct force feedback. Then, design a vector-valued multi-DOF USP communication law, introduce extra passivity (gamma_r < -alpha) to offset the shortage, ensuring closed-loop stability. Finally, by offline optimizing communication parameters (impedance b and gain gamma_l), minimize position/force tracking error, improve transparency. The key innovation lies in extending scalar USP to vector multi-DOF, and using LMI to handle stability proof for nonlinear dynamics. Communication channel uses wave variable transformation, connects master and slave via delay line, uses reflection coefficient to regulate energy flow.

实验Experiments

仿真验证了非线性 2-DOF 平面机械臂。设置延迟 T=0.2s，环境为刚性墙。对比了自由运动和硬接触两种模式。结果显示位置轨迹稳定，力反馈能准确传递（RMS 误差约 1.3N）。优化算法用了 patternsearch，找到了 b=0.06, gamma_l=-20。虽然没真机实验，但仿真覆盖了从自由到接触切换的场景，证明了理论推导的有效性。不过仿真环境较理想，没考虑噪声和模型失配。图 3 和图 4 展示了位置和力的跟踪效果，证明了方法在特定参数下的可行性。
Simulation verified nonlinear 2-DOF planar manipulators. Set delay T=0.2s, environment is rigid wall. Compared free motion and hard contact two modes. Results show position trajectory stable, force feedback can be accurately transmitted (RMS error about 1.3N). Optimization algorithm used patternsearch, found b=0.06, gamma_l=-20.
