Orthogonal JEPA: Factorized Predictive States for Latent World Models

Taoyong Cui, Pheng Ann Heng, Wanli Ouyang
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给 JEPA 动了个“分家”手术，试图把独裁的单一目标向量拆成多个“分家”成员。传统的 JEPA 像个独裁者，把所有预测信息塞进一个向量里，结果高方差信号抢了风头，弱信号被淹没。Orthogonal JEPA 试图用正交分解把目标状态拆成多个独立分支预测，听起来很美好，像是要搞“多核并行”。实验覆盖挺广，从视觉到分子动力学，数据量看着不小，但核心创新点其实是在损失函数和矩阵操作上动刀，而不是架构大改。缺点也很明显，正交只是几何约束，不代表语义解耦，而且确定性预测在复杂环境里可能不够鲁棒。总的来说，是给世界模型加了个“多任务学习”的插件，效果有提升，但别指望它能解决所有表征学习的根本矛盾。
This paper performs a 'splitting' surgery on JEPA, attempting to split the dictatorial single target vector into multiple 'split' members. Traditional JEPA acts like a dictator, stuffing all prediction information into a single vector, resulting in high-variance signals stealing the spotlight while weak signals are submerged. Orthogonal JEPA attempts to decompose the target state into multiple independent branch predictions using orthogonal decomposition, which sounds very promising, like aiming for 'multi-core parallelism'. Experiments cover a wide range, from vision to molecular dynamics, with data volume appearing substantial, but the core innovation actually lies in making modifications to the loss function and matrix operations, rather than major architecture changes. Disadvantages are also obvious; orthogonality is just a geometric constraint, not representing semantic decoupling, and deterministic prediction may not be robust enough in complex environments. Overall, it adds a 'multi-task learning' plugin to the world model; effects are improved, but do not expect it to solve all fundamental contradictions of representation learning.

研究动机Motivation

世界模型的核心是构建潜在状态以支持预测和规划 [1][2]。JEPA 通过预测表示空间而非重建像素来学习这种状态，避免了重建噪声的问题 [7]。然而，标准 JEPA 采用单一目标嵌入和预测路径，这在复杂系统中会导致容量分配问题。高方差或易预测的结构可能主导优化，而较弱的预测结构可能收到冲突梯度。这就像让一个全能选手包揽所有任务，反而忽略了专业分工。现有方法如 Cell-JEPA [15] 虽在单细胞领域有效，但仍面临单一体制下的信息竞争。本文旨在解决这种“单点故障”风险，通过因子化预测提升状态表示的丰富性和稳定性，特别是在多尺度、多实体交互的场景下。
The core of world models is constructing latent states to support prediction and planning [1][2]. JEPA learns this state by predicting representation space rather than reconstructing pixels, avoiding reconstruction noise issues [7]. However, standard JEPA adopts a single target embedding and prediction path, which leads to capacity allocation problems in complex systems. High-variance or easily predictable structures may dominate optimization, while weaker prediction structures may receive conflicting gradients. This is like letting an all-around athlete take on all tasks, instead ignoring professional division of labor. Existing methods like Cell-JEPA [15] are effective in the single-cell field but still face information competition under a single system. This paper aims to solve this 'single point of failure' risk, enhancing the richness and stability of state representation through factorized prediction, especially in multi-scale, multi-entity interaction scenarios.

方法Method

整体流程分为预测状态接口、正交预测因子化和状态合成。首先，域适配器将观测映射为内容 token，上下文编码器生成 $z_c$，EMA 目标编码器生成 $z_t$。核心创新在于正交预测因子化：引入 $K$ 个学习到的基矩阵 $B_k$，将目标状态 $z_t$ 分解为 $K$ 个分量 $z_t^{(k)}$。每个分量由独立的预测分支 $q_k$ 基于共享上下文进行预测。为了保持方向和大小的完整性，直接回归每个因子。损失函数包含四项：预测损失 $L_{pred}$ 保证回归精度；正交损失 $L_{orth}$ 强制基矩阵列正交，减少冗余；因子活动正则化 $L_{fac}$ 防止投影坐标失效；在线方差正则化 $L_{enc}$ 防止编码器坍缩。最后通过 Moore-Penrose 伪逆合成完整状态 $b z_t$。这种设计将预测机制与观测几何分离，同一机制可应用于时序预测、空间隐藏区域或生物系统。
The overall process is divided into prediction state interface, orthogonal prediction factorization, and state synthesis. First, the domain adapter maps observations to content tokens, context encoder generates $z_c$, EMA target encoder generates $z_t$. The core innovation lies in orthogonal prediction factorization: introducing $K$ learned basis matrices $B_k$, decomposing target state $z_t$ into $K$ components $z_t^{(k)}$. Each component is predicted by independent prediction branches $q_k$ based on shared context. To maintain the integrity of direction and magnitude, directly regress each factor. Loss function contains four terms: prediction loss $L_{pred}$ ensures regression accuracy; orthogonal loss $L_{orth}$ forces basis matrix columns to be orthogonal, reducing redundancy; factor activity regularization $L_{fac}$ prevents projection coordinates from failing; online variance regularization $L_{enc}$ prevents encoder collapse. Finally synthesize complete state $b z_t$ via Moore-Penrose pseudo-inverse. This design separates prediction mechanism from observation geometry, the same mechanism can be applied to temporal prediction, spatial hidden regions, or biological systems.

实验Experiments

实验涵盖五大领域：视觉绑定、单细胞转录组、健康记录预测、连续控制和分子动力学。基线包括冻结预训练、标准 JEPA、传统 ML 模型及 autoregressive 模型。视觉任务中，Orthogonal JEPA 在注入准确率 (INJ) 和恢复率上优于标准 JEPA。单细胞聚类 AvgBIO 从 0.7830 提升至 0.8001。健康预测 PRAUC 达到 0.718，优于 XGBoost 和 Prophet。控制任务中，Walker2d 回报从 -11.2 大幅提升至 45.1。分子动力学 RMSD 误差也略有降低。消融实验表明正交性和正则化项对性能至关重要。结果证明因子化预测在不同模态下均能提升表征质量和长视界稳定性，但讨论部分也承认正交性不保证语义独立性。
Experiments cover five major domains: visual binding, single-cell transcriptomics, health record prediction, continuous control, and molecular dynamics. Baselines include frozen pre-training, standard JEPA, traditional ML models, and autoregressive models. In visual tasks, Orthogonal JEPA outperforms standard JEPA in injection accuracy (INJ) and recovery rate. Single-cell clustering AvgBIO improved from 0.7830 to 0.8001. Health prediction PRAUC reached 0.718, outperforming XGBoost and Prophet. In control tasks, Walker2d return significantly increased from -11.2 to 45.1. Molecular dynamics RMSD error also decreased slightly. Ablation studies show orthogonality and regularization terms are crucial for performance. Results prove factorized prediction can improve representation quality and long-horizon stability across different modalities, but the discussion section also admits orthogonality does not guarantee semantic independence.
