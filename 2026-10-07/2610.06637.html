Long-Horizon Textual World Modeling through Structured Reasoning

Fangxin Wang, Xiang Gao, Yuguang Yao, Kaiwen Dong, Nikash Walia, Kamalika Das
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给文本世界模型来了个“结构化推理”的猛药。作者敏锐地指出递归预测误差会像滚雪球一样随 horizon 累积，而直接预测又丢了中间过程导致监督困难。于是搞了个 latent-SR，不递归也不直接跳终点，而是预测“状态补丁”（sparse edits）。这招挺聪明，把中间状态显式化，方便监督，有点像让 LLM 写“思考链”但约束成结构化状态。不过，用 GRPO 做轨迹级强化学习，训练成本估计不低，且依赖蒸馏初始化。虽然长 horizon 表现确实强，但文本状态的可解释性是不是有点“伪科学”？毕竟底层还是 LLM 生成的概率分布。总的来说，在文本 POMDP 里算是个扎实的工作，证明了非递归结构化推理的潜力，但视觉领域能不能迁移还存疑，毕竟文本比像素好编辑多了。
This paper administers a 'potent remedy' of 'structured reasoning' to text world models. The authors keenly point out that recursive prediction errors accumulate like a snowball with horizon, while direct prediction loses intermediate processes, leading to supervision difficulties. Thus, they developed latent-SR, which neither recurses nor jumps directly to the endpoint, but predicts 'state patches' (sparse edits). This strategy is quite clever, making intermediate states explicit for easier supervision, somewhat like asking an LLM to write a 'chain of thought' but constrained into structured states. However, using GRPO for trajectory-level reinforcement learning, the training cost is estimated to be not low, and it relies on distillation initialization. Although the long horizon performance is indeed strong, isn't the interpretability of text states a bit 'pseudo-science'? After all, the underlying layer is still the probability distribution generated by LLMs. Overall, it counts as a solid work in text POMDPs, proving the potential of non-recursive structured reasoning, but whether it can transfer to the visual field is still doubtful, after all, text is much easier to edit than pixels.

研究动机Motivation

世界模型的核心是预测环境演化，但在长 horizon 下困难重重。现有方法分两派：递归式（如 Dreamer [Hafner et al., 2019]）虽然暴露中间状态，但误差会随 horizon 累积，导致预测崩塌；直接多步预测（如 Asadi et al., 2019）避免了递归反馈，但难以监督中间过程，且不同 horizon 难以共享学习，容易丢失历史信息。在部分可观测文本环境中，模型需从历史中压缩信息，既要保留有用事实又要跟踪动作变化。现有方法要么丢失信息，要么无法有效定位错误。本文旨在解决长 horizon 预测中的误差传播与中间监督缺失问题，通过结构化推理让中间状态可被显式检查和奖励，从而在单次调用中实现多步推理。
The core of world models is predicting environment evolution, but it is fraught with difficulties under long horizons. Existing methods are divided into two schools: recursive (e.g., Dreamer [Hafner et al., 2019]) exposes intermediate states, but errors accumulate with horizon, leading to prediction collapse; Direct multi-step prediction (e.g., Asadi et al., 2019) avoids recursive feedback, but is difficult to supervise intermediate processes, and learning is hard to share across different horizons, easily losing historical information. In partially observable text environments, models need to compress information from history, retaining useful facts while tracking action changes. Existing methods either lose information or fail to effectively locate errors. This paper aims to solve the error propagation and lack of intermediate supervision problems in long horizon prediction, allowing intermediate states to be explicitly checked and rewarded through structured reasoning, thereby achieving multi-step reasoning in a single call.

方法Method

整体框架分为编码、结构化推理转换、解码。输入历史得到初始状态 Z_t，SR 转换模块不直接生成状态，而是输出动作对齐的稀疏编辑 Delta_1:k。中间状态通过确定性 Apply 算子从初始状态和编辑序列重构，不反馈回模型，避免了递归误差。训练分两阶段：先用教师策略蒸馏高质量轨迹初始化，防止早期训练信号稀疏；再用 GRPO 进行轨迹级强化学习。奖励函数包含三部分：终端预测质量、相对于历史基线的预测增益（predictive gain）、以及中间状态预测质量。这种设计让模型在单次调用中完成多步推理，同时通过中间奖励定位错误，避免了递归反馈带来的误差放大，且稀疏编辑减少了生成负担。
The overall framework is divided into encoding, structured reasoning transformation, and decoding. Input history yields initial state Z_t, the SR transformation module does not directly generate states, but outputs action-aligned sparse edits Delta_1:k. Intermediate states are reconstructed from the initial state and edit sequence via a deterministic Apply operator, not fed back to the model, avoiding recursive errors. Training is divided into two stages: first, distill high-quality trajectories with a teacher policy for initialization to prevent sparse early training signals; then use GRPO for trajectory-level reinforcement learning. The reward function contains three parts: terminal prediction quality, predictive gain relative to historical baseline, and intermediate state prediction quality. This design allows the model to complete multi-step reasoning in a single call, while locating errors through intermediate rewards, avoiding error amplification brought by recursive feedback, and sparse edits reduce generation burden.

实验Experiments

在 SCIENCEWORLD、JERICHO 和 CEO-BENCH 三个部分可观测文本环境中评估。基线包括递归预测、直接预测及 GPT-5.2 等。指标有 Fact-F1、BLEU、SE-score 等。结果显示 latent-SR 在长 horizon（如 h10）下显著优于递归和直接基线，SCIENCEWORLD 相对提升 12.3%，JERICHO 误差下降近 30%。反事实敏感性测试中，它是唯一对动作序列有显著统计敏感性的模型，证明其真正理解了动作因果。消融实验表明，蒸馏初始化和中间状态奖励对性能提升至关重要，且稀疏编辑比全状态再生更有效。此外，状态扰动实验显示其鲁棒性更强，错误不易传播。
Evaluated in three partially observable text environments: SCIENCEWORLD, JERICHO, and CEO-BENCH. Baselines include recursive prediction, direct prediction, and GPT-5.2, etc. Metrics include Fact-F1, BLEU, SE-score, etc. Results show latent-SR significantly outperforms recursive and direct baselines under long horizon (e.g., h10), SCIENCEWORLD relative improvement 12.3%, JERICHO error drop nearly 30%. In counterfactual sensitivity tests, it is the only model with significant statistical sensitivity to action sequences, proving it truly understands action causality. Ablation studies show that distillation initialization and intermediate state rewards are crucial for performance improvement, and sparse edits are more effective than full state regeneration. Additionally, state perturbation experiments show it has stronger robustness, errors are not easily propagated.
