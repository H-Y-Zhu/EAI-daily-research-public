The Unexpired Plan: A Free Monitor for Accelerated Diffusion Policies

Yi Zhao, Sebastian Scherer
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给训练无加速的扩散策略泼了一盆冷水：原来那些自证清白的“门控”机制，不过是加速器的自嗨，在闭环任务中几乎全是幻觉。作者用114种配置和4个策略家族证明，传统的特征距离监控不仅没用，甚至不如随机猜。最讽刺的是，即便给一个全知全能的“神谕”监控，只要它允许“重试”，照样会崩盘。本文的“未过期计划”确实是个聪明的免费午餐，利用分块策略中天然存在的冗余计划作为参考，既省算力又保安全。但别高兴太早，这招对纯分块策略有效，一旦策略变成单步输出，这“免费午餐”就没了。而且，这方法在仿真里跑得很欢，真机上的表现？作者诚实地说“还没测”，毕竟仿真里的接触丰富任务（如ManiSkill3）已经让很多加速方案原形毕露了。
This paper pours cold water on training-free diffusion policies: it turns out those self-exonerating "gating" mechanisms are merely the accelerator's self-indulgence, almost entirely hallucinations in closed-loop tasks. The authors demonstrate with 114 configurations and 4 strategy families that traditional feature distance monitoring is not only useless but even worse than random guessing. Most ironically, even with an omniscient "Oracle" monitor, as long as it allows "retries", it will still crash. This paper's "Unexpired Plan" is indeed a clever free lunch, utilizing naturally existing redundant plans in chunked policies as references, saving compute while ensuring safety. But don't celebrate too early; this trick works for pure chunked policies, but once the policy becomes single-step output, this "free lunch" is gone. Moreover, this method runs very well in simulation, how about performance on real robots? The authors honestly say "not yet tested", after all, contact-rich tasks in simulation (like ManiSkill3) have already exposed many acceleration schemes.

研究动机Motivation

扩散策略（Diffusion Policies）在机器人控制中表现优异，但推理成本高，导致训练无加速（Training-free Acceleration）成为热点。现有加速方法（如缓存特征、跳过去噪步、重用动作块）普遍依赖“门控”机制来判断何时可以复用缓存。然而，这些门控通常基于特征空间的一致性（Feature Agreement）或外部信号，被证明是闭环成功率的弱预测因子。文献[1]指出，特征空间的一致性无法保证闭环稳定性；文献[13]也表明，门控的溯源（Provenance）决定了可靠性，但现有工作往往只关注信号本身，忽略了“响应”策略（Response Strategy）的关键作用。本文发现，现有的监控方案存在致命缺陷：加速器往往“自证清白”，即用自己的缓存和阈值互相验证，导致在闭环中失效（如LIBERO-10任务中成功率从97%暴跌至6%）。此外，即使使用完美的局部误差信号，如果响应策略允许“重试”（Re-arming），误差累积仍会导致灾难。因此，亟需一种既免费（无需额外前向传播）又能真正反映闭环风险、且具备“不可遗忘”响应机制的监控方案。
Diffusion Policies (Diffusion Policies) perform excellently in robot control, but inference costs are high, making Training-free Acceleration (Training-free Acceleration) a hot topic. Existing acceleration methods (such as caching features, skipping denoising steps, reusing action chunks) generally rely on "gating" mechanisms to determine when caches can be reused. However, these gates are usually based on Feature Agreement (Feature Agreement) in feature space or external signals, proven to be weak predictors of closed-loop success rates. Literature [1] points out that consistency in feature space cannot guarantee closed-loop stability; Literature [13] also indicates that the Provenance (Provenance) of gating determines reliability, but existing work often focuses only on the signal itself, ignoring the key role of the "Response" Strategy (Response Strategy). This paper finds that existing monitoring schemes have fatal flaws: accelerators often "self-exonerate", i.e., verify each other using their own caches and thresholds, leading to failure in closed-loop (e.g., success rate plummeting from 97% to 6% in LIBERO-10 tasks). Furthermore, even with perfect local error signals, if the response strategy allows "retries" (Re-arming), error accumulation will still lead to disaster. Therefore, there is an urgent need for a monitoring scheme that is both free (no additional Forward Pass (Forward Pass)), truly reflects closed-loop risk, and possesses an "unforgettable" response mechanism.

方法Method

本文提出“未过期计划”（The Unexpired Plan）监控框架，核心思想是利用分块策略（Chunked Policy）中天然存在的“未过期”参考计划，无需额外计算即可监控加速器的有效性。整体流程如下：在控制调用t，策略π生成动作块A_t（长度H），仅执行前H_exec步。由于H > H_exec，剩余的H - H_exec步构成了未来m-1次调用的“未过期计划”。

1. 免费监控信号（δ_free）：对于接下来的m-1次调用，直接比较加速器生成的动作与“未过期计划”中对应索引的动作。由于该计划是上一轮参考评估的结果，比较过程无需新的前向传播（Forward Pass），实现了“免费”监控。公式(2)定义了这种偏差度量。
2. 重复检查（λ）：针对加速器可能重复输出相同动作块的情况（导致δ_free失效），引入第二个免费检查λ，计算当前动作与过去K个动作块的最小距离。这能捕捉到策略“停滞”或“死循环”的情况。
3. 响应策略（Absorbing Veto）：这是关键创新。一旦监控触发（Veto），加速器被“吸收”（Retire），即在该回合内永久降级为更保守的配置（如直接运行参考策略），不再允许“重试”或“重武装”。这种“不可遗忘”的机制防止了误差累积。
4. 未覆盖调用处理：每m次调用中，第m次调用没有未过期计划，此时必须运行参考策略并执行，作为基准。这种设计确保了100%的覆盖率，同时通过公式(3)计算了有效加速比S_eff。
5. 候选选择与恢复：系统维护一个加速配置“梯子”（Ladder），按成本排序。当Veto触发时，系统降级到下一层配置，直到耗尽或恢复。
This paper proposes the "Unexpired Plan" (The Unexpired Plan) monitoring framework, the core idea is to utilize the naturally existing "Unexpired" reference plans in Chunked Policy (Chunked Policy) to monitor accelerator effectiveness without additional computation.

The overall process is as follows: at control call t, policy π generates action chunk A_t (length H), only executing the first H_exec steps.

Since H > H_exec, the remaining H - H_exec steps constitute the "Unexpired Plan" for the future m-1 calls.

1. Free Monitoring Signal (δ_free): For the next m-1 calls, directly compare the actions generated by the accelerator with the actions at corresponding indices in the "Unexpired Plan". Since this plan is the result of the previous round's reference evaluation, the comparison process requires no new Forward Pass (Forward Pass), achieving "free" monitoring. Formula (2) defines this deviation metric.
2. Redundancy Check (λ): Addressing the case where the accelerator may repeatedly output the same action chunk (causing δ_free to fail), a second free check λ is introduced, calculating the minimum distance between current actions and the past K action chunks. This can capture situations where the policy is "stagnant" or in a "dead loop".
3. Response Strategy (Absorbing Veto): This is the key innovation. Once monitoring triggers (Veto), the accelerator is "Retired" (Retire), i.e., permanently downgraded to a more conservative configuration within that round (such as directly running the reference policy), no longer allowing "retries" or "re-arming". This "unforgettable" mechanism prevents error accumulation.
4. Uncovered Call Handling: Among every m calls, the m-th call has no Unexpired Plan, at this time the reference policy must be run and executed as a baseline. This design ensures 100% coverage, while calculating the effective acceleration ratio S_eff via Formula (3).
5. Candidate Selection & Recovery: The system maintains an acceleration configuration "Ladder" (Ladder), sorted by cost. When Veto triggers, the system downgrades to the next layer configuration, until exhausted or recovered.

实验Experiments

实验在LIBERO、LIBERO-10、PushT、MetaWorld及ManiSkill3（第五个未见过的家族）上进行，共114种配置。基线包括特征缓存、分块检索、学习容差、均匀步长减少等。评测指标为闭环成功率（ΔSR）和有效加速比（S_eff）。

主要结果：
1. 现有监控失效：传统门控（如特征一致性）在闭环中几乎全部失败，甚至不如随机复用。即使使用“神谕”监控（已知所有前向传播和局部误差），若采用“重试”策略，成功率仍下降20点。
2. 未过期计划有效：本文提出的方法在四个家族中均能保持±2点的误差范围内，加速比达到1.55x-3.09x。特别是在ManiSkill3（接触丰富任务）中，其他方案完全失效，而本文方法成功将任务拉回安全范围。
3. 响应策略关键：消融实验表明，将“重试”改为“吸收”（Absorbing）是成功的关键，仅改变统计量而无正确响应策略无法解决问题。
4. 泛化性：在未见过的ManiSkill3任务上，无需调整阈值即可生效，证明了方法的鲁棒性。

局限性：目前仅在仿真中验证，未涉及真实机器人；加速比上限受分块长度m限制。
Experiments were conducted on LIBERO, LIBERO-10, PushT, MetaWorld, and ManiSkill3 (the fifth unseen family), totaling 114 configurations. Baselines include feature caching, chunk retrieval, learned tolerance, uniform step reduction, etc. Evaluation metrics are closed-loop success rate (ΔSR) and effective acceleration ratio (S_eff).

Main Results:
1. Existing Monitoring Failure: Traditional gating (such as feature consistency) almost entirely fails in closed-loop, even worse than random reuse. Even using "Oracle" monitoring (knowing all Forward Passes (Forward Pass) and local errors), if "retry" strategy is adopted, success rate still drops by 20 points.
2. Unexpired Plan Effective: The method proposed in this paper maintains within ±2 points error range across four families, acceleration ratio reaches 1.55x-3.09x. Especially in ManiSkill3 (contact-rich tasks), other schemes completely fail, while this paper's method successfully pulls the task back to a safe range.
3. Response Strategy Key: Ablation studies show that changing "retry" to "Absorbing" (Absorbing) is the key to success, merely changing statistics without correct response strategy cannot solve the problem.
4. Generalization: On unseen ManiSkill3 tasks, it works without adjusting thresholds, proving the method's robustness.

Limitations: Currently verified only in simulation, not involving real robots; acceleration ratio upper limit is limited by chunk length m.
