SAGE: Subgoal-Conditioned Action Generation for Latent World Model Planning

Letian Cheng, Qi Zhang, Yisen Wang
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给冷冰冰的潜世界模型加了个“导航仪”。SAGE 的核心思路其实不新鲜：长任务拆短跑。但亮点在于它没瞎猜，而是先预测个“可达的中转站”（subgoal），再基于这个中转站生成动作。相比那些还在随机撒网找动作的基线，这招确实让长视野规划活过来了。在 H=150 的极端测试下，PushT 成功率从 12.7% 飙升至 64.7%，数据很能打。不过，这方法有点“借鸡生蛋”，完全依赖 LeWM 的冻结权重，如果世界模型本身预测不准，subgoal 生成器再努力也是空中楼阁。另外，实验主要集中在 PushT 和 Cube，这种接触式操作任务对局部目标比较敏感，换到纯导航或者更复杂的物理交互，subgoal 的“可达性”假设会不会崩？总之，工程上很稳，但理论上的泛化性还得打个问号。
This paper adds a "navigator" to the cold latent world models. The core idea of SAGE is actually not new: breaking long tasks into short runs. But the highlight is that it doesn't guess blindly, but first predicts a "reachable waypoint" (subgoal), and then generates actions based on this waypoint. Compared to baselines that are still randomly casting nets to find actions, this approach indeed brings long-horizon planning back to life. Under extreme testing at H=150, PushT success rate soared from 12.7% to 64.7%, the data is very compelling. However, this method is a bit like "borrowing a chicken to lay an egg", completely relying on LeWM's frozen weights; if the world model itself predicts inaccurately, no matter how hard the subgoal generator works, it is a castle in the air. Additionally, experiments focus mainly on PushT and Cube; these contact manipulation tasks are sensitive to local goals; switching to pure navigation or more complex physical interactions, will the "reachability" assumption of subgoals collapse? In short, it is very stable in engineering, but theoretical generalization still needs a question mark.

研究动机Motivation

潜世界模型（Latent World Models）如 LeWM (Maes et al., 2026) 和 DINO-WM (Zhou et al., 2025) 虽然能通过隐空间预测加速规划，但在长视野任务中面临“提议质量”瓶颈。随着规划 horizon 增长，动作空间呈指数级扩大，固定候选预算下，随机初始化（如 Base CEM）很难覆盖到高质量的未来轨迹。现有工作如 PRISM (Wang et al., 2026) 虽然引入了状态条件提议，但缺乏任务结构引导。SAGE 旨在解决这一矛盾：当目标太远时，直接优化最终目标信号微弱，需要引入可达的中间状态（subgoal）来分解长程任务，平衡局部控制与长程进展，从而让冻结的世界模型能评估到更合理的候选未来。
Latent World Models (Latent World Models) such as LeWM (Maes et al., 2026) and DINO-WM (Zhou et al., 2025) can accelerate planning through latent space prediction, but face a "proposal quality" bottleneck in long-horizon tasks. As planning horizon grows, action space expands exponentially; under fixed candidate budget, random initialization (such as Base CEM) struggles to cover high-quality future trajectories. Existing work such as PRISM (Wang et al., 2026) introduces state-conditioned proposals, but lacks task structure guidance. SAGE aims to resolve this contradiction: when the goal is too far, directly optimizing the final goal signal is weak; reachable intermediate states (subgoals) need to be introduced to decompose long-range tasks, balancing local control and long-range progress, thereby allowing the frozen world model to evaluate more reasonable candidate futures.

方法Method

SAGE 框架包含两个核心训练组件和一个冻结评估器。整体流程是：输入当前历史与远端目标 -> 子目标生成器预测局部潜状态 -> 动作生成器基于子目标生成动作选项 -> 冻结 LeWM 评估并 CEM 优化。
1. **子目标生成器 (Subgoal Generator)**: 使用 Transformer Decoder，输入包括历史 tokens、远端目标、剩余 offset 和请求时长 τ。它预测在 τ 步后的可达潜状态，公式为远端目标潜状态加上残差项，确保输出在 LeWM 空间内。支持多尺度 τ（15, 20, 25 步），兼顾局部细节与长程进度。训练时使用 SmoothL1 和余弦损失对齐 LeWM 潜状态。
2. **子目标条件动作生成器 (Action Generator)**: 同样基于 Transformer，输入包含预测的子目标。它输出轨迹级高斯混合模型（GMM），每个高斯代表一个动作选项。通过 duration mask 激活对应长度的查询，确保动作与子目标时长匹配。训练时优化轨迹混合负对数似然。
3. **评估与优化**: 生成器产生的动作选项初始化 CEM 种群，冻结的 LeWM 根据预测的未来潜状态与子目标的距离计算代价，筛选精英集并拟合高斯分布进行迭代优化，最后执行首段动作。
The SAGE framework includes two core training components and one frozen evaluator. The overall process is: input current history and distant goal -> subgoal generator predicts local latent state -> action generator generates action options based on subgoal -> frozen LeWM evaluates and CEM optimizes.
1. **Subgoal Generator (Subgoal Generator)**: Uses Transformer Decoder, inputs include history tokens, distant goal, remaining offset, and requested duration τ. It predicts reachable latent states after τ steps, formula is distant goal latent state plus residual term, ensuring output is within LeWM space. Supports multi-scale τ (15, 20, 25 steps), balancing local details and long-range progress. During training, SmoothL1 and cosine loss are used to align LeWM latent states.
2. **Subgoal-Conditioned Action Generator (Action Generator)**: Also based on Transformer, input includes predicted subgoal. It outputs trajectory-level Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM), each Gaussian represents an action option. Activates queries of corresponding length through duration mask, ensuring actions match subgoal duration. During training, optimizes trajectory mixture negative log-likelihood.
3. **Evaluation and Optimization**: Action options generated by the generator initialize CEM population, frozen LeWM calculates cost based on distance between predicted future latent states and subgoal, filters elite set and fits Gaussian distribution for iterative optimization, finally executes first segment of actions.

实验Experiments

在 PushT 和 OGBench Cube 上测试，使用冻结的 LeWM 权重。基线包括 Base CEM、PRISM、Generator-only 等。指标为成功率。
主要结果：在 H=150 时，PushT 成功率从 12.7% 提升至 64.7%，Cube 从 26.7% 提升至 67.3%。
消融实验表明：仅生成子目标（Generator-only）已能显著提升性能；动作生成器进一步约束候选分布；LeWM 引导的 CEM refinement 对长视野至关重要（SAGE prior top 在 H=150 仅 16%）。
时间调度研究：更细粒度的规划阶段（如 15 步 vs 25 步）和顺序影响最终性能，证明中间状态的质量很关键。实验使用了 3 个固定种子（32, 42, 52）的 held-out manifests 进行验证，确保结果稳健。
Tested on PushT and OGBench Cube, using frozen LeWM weights. Baselines include Base CEM, PRISM, Generator-only, etc. Metric is success rate.
Main results: At H=150, PushT success rate improved from 12.7% to 64.7%, Cube from 26.7% to 67.3%.
Ablation studies show: generating subgoals only (Generator-only) already significantly improves performance; action generator further constrains candidate distribution; LeWM-guided CEM refinement is crucial for long-horizon (SAGE prior top at H=150 only 16%).
Time scheduling study: Finer-grained planning stages (e.g., 15 steps vs 25 steps) and order affect final performance, proving quality of intermediate states is key. Experiments used 3 fixed seeds (32, 42, 52) held-out manifests for verification, ensuring results are robust.
