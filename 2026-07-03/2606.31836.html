RoboTacDex: A Dexterous Visual-Tactile-Action Dataset for Humanoid Manipulation

Xinyi Wang, Donghan Li, Zi'Ang Chen, Chong Yu, Chen Xin, Peng Ye
abspdf

论文点评Review

人形机器人终于有了带“触觉”的专属数据集，RoboTacDex 试图用 6k 轨迹填补这一空白。但对比 Open X-Embodiment 的百万级数据，这点量级更像是“精致小灶”而非“自助餐”。最讽刺的是，作者花大力气搞了四视角同步和触觉反馈，结果实验发现现有模型（ACT/DP）直接堆多视角没啥用，VLA 模型虽然强但也吃数据。这说明光有数据不够，还得有能消化多模态的架构。不过，至少触觉数据让“拧瓶盖”这种任务少了一些“空转”失败，算是给触觉派争了口气。数据集开源是好事，但希望后续能跟上规模，别让人形机器人还在“婴儿期”打转，毕竟 6k 数据教不出真正的通用智能。
Humanoid robots finally have a dedicated dataset with "tactile" capabilities; RoboTacDex attempts to fill this gap with 6k trajectories. However, compared to the million-level data of Open X-Embodiment, this scale resembles "fine dining" rather than a "buffet". Ironically, despite the authors' significant efforts in implementing four-view synchronization and tactile feedback, experiments revealed that existing models (ACT/DP) derive little benefit from simply stacking multi-view inputs, while VLA models, though powerful, remain data-hungry. This indicates that data alone is insufficient; an architecture capable of processing multimodal information is also required. Nevertheless, at least the tactile data reduced "idling" failures in tasks such as "unscrewing bottle caps," offering some vindication for tactile-based approaches. While open-sourcing the dataset is positive, it is hoped that future iterations will match the scale, preventing humanoid robots from remaining stuck in the "infancy" stage, as 6k data cannot cultivate true general intelligence.

研究动机Motivation

人形机器人操作领域长期面临高质量数据集匮乏的困境。虽然固定基座机械臂的数据集已相对成熟 [1]-[3]，但针对人形机器人的数据极其稀缺。现有的人形数据集如 RoboMIND [3] 和 Agibot World [42] 虽规模较大，却普遍缺失触觉反馈或仅依赖单视角视觉，难以支撑复杂物理交互。人形机器人自由度（DOF）高，双臂协调任务复杂，且需在非结构化环境中操作，这对数据的多样性、系统完整性提出了更高要求。纯视觉输入往往不足以处理遮挡和精细接触，多视角深度和触觉信息成为实现灵巧操作的关键。因此，构建融合视觉、深度和触觉的人形操作数据集，是迈向通用具身智能的关键基石。
The field of humanoid robot manipulation has long struggled with a scarcity of high-quality datasets. While datasets for fixed-base manipulators are relatively mature [1]-[3], data specifically for humanoid robots remains extremely scarce. Existing humanoid datasets, such as RoboMIND [3] and Agibot World [42], though large in scale, generally lack tactile feedback or rely solely on single-view vision, making them inadequate for supporting complex physical interactions. Humanoid robots possess high degrees of freedom (DOF), involve complex bimanual coordination tasks, and must operate in unstructured environments, imposing higher demands on data diversity and system integrity. Pure visual input is often insufficient to handle occlusion and fine contact; thus, multi-view depth and tactile information are critical for achieving dexterous manipulation. Therefore, constructing a humanoid manipulation dataset that integrates vision, depth, and tactile sensing is a key cornerstone for advancing towards general embodied intelligence.

方法Method

整体流程基于遥操作数据采集与多模态同步。系统以 Unitree G1 人形机器人为载体，操作员佩戴 Meta Horizon VR 映射上半身动作。数据采集核心在于解决异构传感器同步问题：头部与第三人称视角采用 RealSense D435i 硬件同步，手腕摄像头则通过软件同步消除时间错位，确保多视角几何与语义一致性。触觉数据由 Brainco Revo2 灵巧手以 100Hz 采集，与 30Hz 的视觉及关节状态数据对齐。数据集涵盖 19 类任务、23 种技能及 22 种物体，包含 RGB、深度、触觉及语义标注。此外，研究还重写了遥操作脚本以支持深度与触觉记录，并构建了包含 ACT、Diffusion Policy 及 GROOT N1.5 的验证基准，用于评估不同策略在高维人形动作空间下的表现，确保数据质量与可用性。
The overall workflow is based on teleoperation data collection and multimodal synchronization. The system utilizes the Unitree G1 humanoid robot as the platform, with operators wearing Meta Horizon VR to map upper-body movements. The core of data collection addresses heterogeneous sensor synchronization: the head and third-person views employ RealSense D435i hardware synchronization, while wrist cameras use software synchronization to eliminate temporal misalignment, ensuring geometric and semantic consistency across multi-views. Tactile data is collected by the Brainco Revo2 dexterous hand at 100Hz, aligned with 30Hz vision and joint state data. The dataset encompasses 19 task categories, 23 skills, and 22 object types, including RGB, depth, tactile, and semantic annotations. Furthermore, the study rewrote teleoperation scripts to support depth and tactile recording and constructed a validation benchmark comprising ACT, Diffusion Policy, and GROOT N1.5 to evaluate the performance of different strategies within the high-dimensional humanoid action space, ensuring data quality and usability.

实验Experiments

在 4 个代表性任务上评估了 ACT、Diffusion Policy 和 GROOT N1.5。结果显示 VLA 模型在简单任务（如 PickAndPlacePear）上成功率最高，得益于预训练先验，但在复杂语义任务（如 InsertBook）上仍受限于数据量。ACT 和 DP 因缺乏多模态融合机制，单纯增加多视角输入并未显著提升成功率，反而因动作空间高维导致插值困难。消融实验表明，触觉反馈虽未直接提升成功率，但改变了失败模式，将“空转”失败转化为“卡住/调整”失败，证明触觉能揭示视觉模糊的接触状态。这验证了多模态数据的价值，但也指出当前模型架构对多视角信息的利用尚不充分。
ACT, Diffusion Policy, and GROOT N1.5 were evaluated on 4 representative tasks. Results indicate that VLA models achieved the highest success rates on simple tasks (e.g., PickAndPlacePear), benefiting from pre-training priors, yet remained limited by data volume on complex semantic tasks (e.g., InsertBook). ACT and DP, lacking multimodal fusion mechanisms, did not see significant success rate improvements from simply adding multi-view inputs, instead facing interpolation difficulties due to the high-dimensional action space. Ablation studies revealed that while tactile feedback did not directly improve success rates, it altered failure modes, converting "idling" failures into "stuck/adjusting" failures, demonstrating that tactile sensing can reveal contact states ambiguous to vision. This validates the value of multimodal data but also highlights that current model architectures do not yet fully utilize multi-view information.
