Multi-scale Mixture of World Models for Embodied Agents in Evolving Environments

Jinwoo Jang, Daniel J. Rho, Sihyung Yoon, Hyunsuk Cho, Honguk Woo
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给具身智能的“多专家混合”（MoE）架构强行加了一层“认知滤镜”。作者敏锐地指出，现有 MoE 路由太粗糙，分不清什么时候该用低层物理模型，什么时候该用高层抽象规则，导致无法针对性更新。MuSix 框架试图用“经验距离”量化环境新颖性，把认知心理学里的解释水平理论（CLT）硬套到了路由机制上，这招确实巧妙，让路由决策显式关联到尺度。不过，搞这么复杂的两阶段路由和跨尺度门控，计算开销和训练稳定性恐怕是个坑。实验数据在仿真上确实好看，但在真实机器人 Franka 上表现尚可但略逊最强基线 SayCanPay（Table 3），说明仿真到真实的鸿沟依然存在。总的来说，是个理论包装得很漂亮的工程尝试，但“多尺度”到底是不是银弹，还得看后续能不能扛住更复杂的动态环境，毕竟把 CLT 量化成数学公式这事儿，本身就有过拟合风险。
This paper forcibly adds a layer of "cognitive filter" to the "Mixture of Experts" (MoE) architecture of embodied intelligence. The authors keenly point out that existing MoE routing is too coarse, failing to distinguish when to use low-level physical models versus high-level abstract rules, resulting in an inability to update targetedly. The MuSix framework attempts to quantify environmental novelty using "experiential distance", forcibly applying the Construal Level Theory (CLT) from cognitive psychology to the routing mechanism; this approach is indeed clever, making routing decisions explicitly associated with scales. However, implementing such complex two-stage routing and cross-scale gating, the computational overhead and training stability are likely pitfalls. Experimental data looks good in simulation, but on the real robot Franka, performance is passable yet slightly inferior to the strongest baseline SayCanPay (Table 3), indicating the sim-to-real gap still exists. Overall, it is an engineering attempt with very beautiful theoretical packaging, but whether "multi-scale" is truly a silver bullet depends on whether it can withstand more complex dynamic environments later, after all, quantifying CLT into mathematical formulas itself carries overfitting risks.

研究动机Motivation

具身智能体在现实世界部署时，面临环境非平稳（non-stationary）的严峻挑战。现有的基于 VLM 的方法 [7, 8, 11] 往往依赖固定假设或单体模型，难以针对性适应新环境，一旦环境变化往往需要全系统重训。虽然 MoE [9] 能通过专家选择缓解灾难性遗忘，但传统 MoE 缺乏“尺度”感知（scale-aware）。这导致两个核心问题：一是路由无法明确区分低层物理动态与高层抽象规则，无法在测试时针对性更新特定尺度知识（Mixture Challenge）；二是统一更新策略无法适应不同尺度知识的过时速率，低层知识变化快，高层规则相对稳定，强行统一更新会导致知识冲突或遗忘（Evolution Challenge）。因此，需要一种能显式区分尺度并允许差异化演化的世界模型混合框架，以平衡灵活性与稳定性。
When deployed in the real world, embodied agents face severe challenges of non-stationary environments. Existing VLM-based methods [7, 8, 11] often rely on fixed assumptions or single models, making it difficult to adapt targetedly to new environments; once the environment changes, full system retraining is often required. Although MoE [9] can alleviate catastrophic forgetting through expert selection, traditional MoE lacks "scale" awareness (scale-aware). This leads to two core problems: first, routing cannot clearly distinguish low-level physical dynamics from high-level abstract rules, unable to update specific scale knowledge targetedly during testing (Mixture Challenge); second, a unified update strategy cannot adapt to the obsolescence rates of knowledge at different scales; low-level knowledge changes fast, high-level rules are relatively stable, forcing unified updates leads to knowledge conflict or forgetting (Evolution Challenge). Therefore, a world model hybrid framework that can explicitly distinguish scales and allow differential evolution is needed to balance flexibility and stability.

方法Method

MuSix 框架核心在于“尺度感知”的世界模型混合与演化，整体流程分为路由与适应两大部分。
1. **两阶段尺度路由**：首先，Meta-router 基于“经验距离”（experiential distance）计算当前情境相对于历史经验的新颖性，映射到连续尺度空间生成权重函数。这借鉴了解释水平理论（CLT）[16]，将心理距离转化为可量化的尺度选择依据。随后，Base router 结合尺度权重选择具体的世界模型。这种解耦设计使得路由决策显式地关联到特定尺度，避免了传统 MoE 的黑盒路由。
2. **知识适应机制**：针对演化挑战，引入组内（Intra-scale）和组间（Inter-scale）适应。组内适应利用尺度依赖的遗忘率（scale-dependent forgetting rates），让低尺度知识快速刷新，高尺度抽象知识持久保留。组间适应通过门控机制（gated cross-scale transfer）在相邻尺度间传递知识，保持层级一致性。
3. **训练目标**：结合世界模型预测损失与尺度对齐损失，确保经验距离与尺度选择的一致性。损失函数包含对齐项、方差正则项和幅度正则项，防止路由输出发散。
The core of the MuSix framework lies in "scale-aware" world model hybridization and evolution, with the overall process divided into two main parts: routing and adaptation.
1. **Two-stage Scale Routing**: First, the Meta-router calculates the novelty of the current context relative to historical experience based on "experiential distance", mapping to a continuous scale space to generate a weight function. This draws on Construal Level Theory (CLT) [16], converting psychological distance into a quantifiable basis for scale selection. Subsequently, the Base router selects specific world models combined with scale weights. This decoupled design makes routing decisions explicitly associated with specific scales, avoiding the black-box routing of traditional MoE.
2. **Knowledge Adaptation Mechanism**: Addressing the evolution challenge, intra-scale and inter-scale adaptation are introduced. Intra-scale adaptation utilizes scale-dependent forgetting rates, allowing low-scale knowledge to refresh quickly while high-scale abstract knowledge is retained persistently. Inter-scale adaptation transfers knowledge between adjacent scales through a gating mechanism (gated cross-scale transfer), maintaining hierarchical consistency.
3. **Training Objectives**: Combining world model prediction loss and scale alignment loss to ensure consistency between experiential distance and scale selection. The loss function includes alignment terms, variance regularization terms, and magnitude regularization terms to prevent routing output divergence.

实验Experiments

论文在 EmbodiedBench [19] 和 HAZARD [22] 上验证了 MuSix。EmbodiedBench 测试多尺度推理能力（如 EB-Habitat, EB-Navigation），HAZARD 测试动态灾难环境下的适应（火灾、洪水）。基线包括 LLM-Planner [15], SayCanPay [7], FLARE [11] 及传统 MoE。
**结果**：MuSix 在 EB-Habitat 上比 SayCanPay 提升 6.05%，在 HAZARD 火灾场景比 FLARE 提升 1.49%。真实机器人 Franka 实验显示平均成功率 56.2%，略低于 SayCanPay 的 58.4%，但优于其他基线。
**消融**：移除 Meta-router 导致性能大幅下降，证明两阶段路由必要性。移除尺度对齐损失也会显著降低表现。不同世界模型架构变体（Variant I-V）均表现稳定，说明框架通用性强。
**发现**：3D 尺度空间表现优于 1D/2D，且不同轴在不同情境下呈现特异性响应，表明尺度空间能捕捉多维新颖性。
The paper validates MuSix on EmbodiedBench [19] and HAZARD [22]. EmbodiedBench tests multi-scale reasoning capabilities (e.g., EB-Habitat, EB-Navigation), HAZARD tests adaptation under dynamic disaster environments (fire, flood). Baselines include LLM-Planner [15], SayCanPay [7], FLARE [11], and traditional MoE.
**Results**: MuSix improved by 6.05% over SayCanPay on EB-Habitat, and by 1.49% over FLARE in HAZARD fire scenarios. Real robot Franka experiments show an average success rate of 56.2%, slightly lower than SayCanPay's 58.4%, but superior to other baselines.
**Ablation**: Removing Meta-router leads to a significant performance drop, proving the necessity of two-stage routing. Removing scale alignment loss also significantly reduces performance. Different world model architecture variants (Variant I-V) all perform stably, indicating the framework has strong generalizability.
**Findings**: 3D scale space performance is superior to 1D/2D, and different axes show specific responses in different contexts, indicating scale space can capture multi-dimensional novelty.
