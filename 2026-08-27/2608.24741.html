One-Shot Learning from Demonstration of Contact-Rich Robotic Manipulation by Identifying Physical Interactions

A. H. G. Overbeek, H. van der Kooij, M. Vlutters
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文试图用“物理交互显式建模”来解决 LfD 在接触丰富任务中的痛点。作者声称只需单次演示就能复现复杂任务，这点很诱人。相比黑盒的 VLA 或 RL 方法，它的可解释性确实强，能清楚看到何时切换位置/力控制。但缺点也很明显：假设环境静态、物体刚性，且强依赖力觉传感器，这在非结构化环境中是硬伤。对于柔性物体或动态干扰，这套基于准静态假设的框架可能直接失效。不过，在工业装配等特定场景下，这种“人机交互可解释”的思路确实比纯数据驱动更靠谱，值得在接触敏感任务中参考。其核心贡献在于将交互的“何时、何地、何种”显式化，而非隐式学习，这在可解释性上是一大进步，尽管工程落地门槛较高。
This paper attempts to use 'explicit modeling of physical interaction' to address the pain points of LfD in contact-rich tasks. The authors claim that complex tasks can be reproduced with only a single demonstration, which is very appealing. Compared to black-box VLA or RL methods, its interpretability is indeed strong, allowing clear visibility of when to switch between position/force control. However, the drawbacks are also obvious: assuming a static environment and rigid objects, and heavily relying on force sensors, which is a fatal flaw in unstructured environments. For flexible objects or dynamic disturbances, this framework based on quasi-static assumptions may fail directly. However, in specific scenarios such as industrial assembly, this 'interpretable human-robot interaction' approach is indeed more reliable than pure data-driven methods and is worth referencing in contact-sensitive tasks. Its core contribution lies in explicitly defining the 'when, where, and what' of interaction, rather than implicit learning, which is a major advancement in interpretability, although the engineering deployment threshold is high.

研究动机Motivation

现有 LfD 方法大多忽略机器人与环境间的物理交互细节，如接触的建立与断开 [1]。轨迹模型（如 PMP [10], DMP [15]）通常只捕捉信号瞬时属性，缺乏物理意义状态，导致在几何变化未知时鲁棒性差。虽然部分工作尝试建模交互 [20]，但未同时处理复杂顺序任务。此外，通用学习方法需要大量物理数据，而触觉信息往往缺失 [5], [6]。本文旨在填补可解释的少样本 LfD 空白，通过显式建模交互来提升任务复现的适应性，减少对大规模数据集的依赖，解决传统方法难以处理接触约束切换的问题。特别是对于制造和装配场景，物理约束的显式处理比黑盒优化更安全。
Existing LfD methods mostly ignore physical interaction details between the robot and the environment, such as contact establishment and disconnection [1]. Trajectory models (such as PMP [10], DMP [15]) usually only capture instantaneous signal attributes, lacking physical state semantics, leading to poor robustness when geometric changes are unknown. Although some work attempts to model interaction [20], it does not handle complex sequential tasks simultaneously. Furthermore, general learning methods require large amounts of physical data, while tactile information is often missing [5], [6]. This paper aims to fill the gap in interpretable few-shot LfD, improve task reproduction adaptability through explicit interaction modeling, reduce dependence on large-scale datasets, and solve the problem that traditional methods find difficult to handle contact constraint switching. Especially for manufacturing and assembly scenarios, explicit handling of physical constraints is safer than black-box optimization.

方法Method

整体流程分为任务建模、过渡条件推导、任务复现控制。首先，从演示数据（位姿、twist、wrench）中分割时间片段，识别交互帧（interaction frame）和交互类（idle, free, constrained, impeded），并扩展为 12 类以支持单侧交互。其次，根据交互类变化推导过渡条件（接触建立/断开）。控制阶段，基于交互类选择位置或力控制，并监控过渡条件。若接触未发生，则进行探索（轨迹外推）。几何变化已知时，通过偏移或轨迹混合进行泛化。未知时，在线适应。核心在于利用交互帧将任务解耦，使控制策略能随物理状态动态调整。通过混合控制器，在约束轴上跟踪力，自由轴上跟踪位姿，实现了物理一致性。
The overall process is divided into task modeling, transition condition derivation, and task reproduction control. First, segment time segments from demonstration data (pose, twist, wrench), identify interaction frame (interaction frame) and interaction classes (idle, free, constrained, impeded), and extend to 12 classes to support unilateral interaction. Second, derive transition conditions (contact establishment/disconnection) based on interaction class changes. In the control phase, select position or force control based on interaction classes, and monitor transition conditions. If contact does not occur, perform exploration (trajectory extrapolation). When geometric changes are known, generalize through offset or trajectory mixing. When unknown, online adaptation. The core lies in decoupling the task using interaction frames, enabling control strategies to dynamically adjust according to physical states. Through a mixed controller, tracking force on constrained axes and pose on free axes achieves physical consistency.

实验Experiments

在 Franka 机器人上评估，任务包括开锁、拧螺丝、表面跟随。仅单次演示。对比纯位置控制、混合控制、接触感知切换、在线适应。结果显示，纯位置控制在几何偏移大时失败率高。加入接触感知切换后，鲁棒性提升。在线适应进一步处理未知偏移。但大偏移仍需离线泛化。证明了显式建模交互的有效性。具体如铰链锁开启，混合控制将成功率从 50% 提升至 70%，在线适应后达 100%。实验验证了该方法在未知几何变化下的鲁棒性，以及已知变化下的泛化能力。通过分步控制器评估，清晰展示了各模块对成功率的贡献。
Evaluated on a Franka robot, tasks include unlocking, screwing, and surface following. Only a single demonstration. Compare pure position control, mixed control, contact-aware switching, and online adaptation. Results show that pure position control has a high failure rate when geometric offsets are large. After adding contact-aware switching, robustness improves. Online adaptation further handles unknown offsets. However, large offsets still require offline generalization. Proves the effectiveness of explicit interaction modeling. Specifically, for hinge lock opening, mixed control increased success rate from 50% to 70%, reaching 100% after online adaptation. Experiments verify the method's robustness under unknown geometric changes and generalization ability under known changes. Through step-wise controller evaluation, the contribution of each module to the success rate is clearly demonstrated.
