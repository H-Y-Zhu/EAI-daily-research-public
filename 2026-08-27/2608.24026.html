NeurRAFT: Robot Motion Planning via Anchor-Level Flow Matching with Clearance-Aware Preference Tuning

Sibo Tian, Chang Liu, Minghui Zheng, Xiao Liang
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇 NeurRAFT 算是给生成式运动规划打了剂强心针。作者没走寻常路，不堆 dense waypoints，而是搞了个 anchor-level 的 Flow Matching，推理只要两步，还配了 cubic-spline 插值，省算力又保平滑。最骚的是用 DPO 做 clearance-aware tuning，不用额外推理修正，直接改分布。不过，偏好对齐那步还是得靠仿真器里的 privileged 几何信息算 clearance，虽然推理时不依赖，但这算不算“开卷考试”？另外，real-world 零迁移确实香，但 Mesh 重建过滤候选轨迹那步，在真实噪声深度图下稳不稳？总的来说，SOTA 上刷分漂亮，但“无 privileged 信息”的宣称在训练阶段还是打了折扣，属于“训练时开挂，推理时隐身”的典范。
This NeurRAFT paper serves as a strong shot of adrenaline for generative motion planning. The authors did not follow the usual path of stacking dense waypoints, but instead implemented an anchor-level Flow Matching, requiring only two steps for inference, equipped with cubic-spline interpolation, saving computation while ensuring smoothness. The most striking aspect is using DPO for clearance-aware tuning, modifying the distribution directly without extra inference correction. However, the preference alignment step still relies on privileged geometric information in the simulator to calculate clearance, although not dependent during inference, does this count as an "open-book exam"? Additionally, real-world zero-shot transfer is indeed appealing, but is the step of Mesh reconstruction filtering candidate trajectories stable under real noisy depth maps? Overall, the scores on SOTA are impressive, but the claim of "no privileged information" is discounted during the training phase, representing a paradigm of "cheating during training, invisible during inference".

研究动机Motivation

传统规划器（如 PRM [4], RRT [5], CHOMP [8]）依赖显式几何模型，从原始传感器重建难且慢。神经规划器（如 MPNet [12], MπNets [1]）虽端到端，但多步 rollout 误差累积，且缺乏多模态建模。生成式方法（Diffusion [14], [15]）虽能建模多模态，但推理慢。现有方法训练仅用正样本，无法区分“擦碰”与“安全”，导致测试时仍有碰撞残留。NeurRAFT 旨在解决轨迹表示冗余（dense waypoints）和训练信号不足（缺乏负样本监督）的问题，通过紧凑 anchor 表示和偏好对齐提升安全性与效率。
Traditional planners (e.g., PRM [4], RRT [5], CHOMP [8]) rely on explicit geometric models, which are difficult and slow to reconstruct from raw sensors. Neural planners (e.g., MPNet [12], MπNets [1]) are end-to-end, but suffer from multi-step rollout error accumulation and lack multi-modal modeling. Generative methods (Diffusion [14], [15]) can model multi-modality but are slow in inference. Existing methods use only positive samples during training, unable to distinguish "collision" from "safe", leading to residual collisions during testing. NeurRAFT aims to solve the problems of trajectory representation redundancy (dense waypoints) and insufficient training signals (lack of negative sample supervision), improving safety and efficiency through compact anchor representation and preference alignment.

方法Method

整体流程分训练与推理。训练含预训练与偏好对齐。推理分 anchor 生成、插值、过滤。
1. **Anchor-Level Flow Matching**: 轨迹不表示为 H 个 dense waypoints，而是 N 个 anchor (N<<H)。Flow Matching 学习从噪声到 anchor 的速度场，推理仅需 2 步 Euler 积分。边界 anchor 固定为 start/goal。
2. **Jacobian-Weighted Loss**: 传统 loss 在 joint space 均匀加权，NeurRAFT 用 Jacobian 加权，惩罚对 end-effector 位移影响大的 joint 误差，更符合任务空间需求。
3. **Clearance-Aware Preference Tuning**: 利用预训练模型采样生成轨迹，用 privileged 几何信息计算 signed obstacle clearance。构建偏好对（winner/loser），用 DPO [20] 优化策略，将概率质量移向高 clearance 轨迹。此过程离线完成，不增加推理开销。
4. **Inference**: 生成多个 anchor 序列 -> Cubic Spline 插值 -> 基于点云重建 Mesh 进行碰撞过滤 -> 选最短路径或最小穿透深度。
The overall process is divided into training and inference. Training includes pre-training and preference alignment. Inference includes anchor generation, interpolation, and filtering.
1. **Anchor-Level Flow Matching**: Trajectories are not represented as H dense waypoints, but as N anchors (N<<H). Flow Matching learns the velocity field from noise to anchor, requiring only 2-step Euler integration for inference. Boundary anchors are fixed as start/goal.
2. **Jacobian-Weighted Loss**: Traditional loss is uniformly weighted in joint space, NeurRAFT uses Jacobian weighting, penalizing joint errors that have a large impact on end-effector displacement, better aligning with task space requirements.
3. **Clearance-Aware Preference Tuning**: Utilize the pre-trained model to sample and generate trajectories, calculate signed obstacle clearance using privileged geometric information. Construct preference pairs (winner/loser), optimize the policy using DPO [20], shifting probability mass towards high clearance trajectories. This process is completed offline, adding no inference overhead.
4. **Inference**: Generate multiple anchor sequences -> Cubic Spline interpolation -> Collision filtering based on point cloud reconstructed Mesh -> Select shortest path or minimum penetration depth.

实验Experiments

在 MπNets [1] 基准测试，含 tabletop, cubby, dresser 场景。对比 Classical (CHOMP [8], STORM [33]) 与 Neural (MπNets [1], EDMP [16], Cascaded Diffusion [18])。指标为 Success Rate。NeurRAFT 在 Global-Solvable 等 split 上均超 SOTA，如 Global Expert 训练下 Global-Solvable 达 93.61%。消融显示 anchor 数 N=8 性价比最高，偏好 tuning 显著提升成功率。真实 Franka 机械臂零迁移实验验证了 sim-to-real 能力，在噪声深度图下有效。
On the MπNets [1] benchmark, containing tabletop, cubby, dresser scenes. Comparing Classical (CHOMP [8], STORM [33]) and Neural (MπNets [1], EDMP [16], Cascaded Diffusion [18]). The metric is Success Rate. NeurRAFT surpasses SOTA on splits such as Global-Solvable, e.g., reaching 93.61% on Global-Solvable under Global Expert training. Ablation shows anchor count N=8 has the best cost-performance ratio, preference tuning significantly improves success rate. Real Franka robot arm zero-shot transfer experiments verify sim-to-real capability, effective under noisy depth maps.
