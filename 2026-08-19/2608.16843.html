Security of Foundation-Model-Powered Embodied Agents: Attack Surfaces, Attacks, Defenses, and Evaluation

Jiawei Liu, Jiacheng Guo, Tian Zhang, Yiwei Xu, Juan Wang, Jinlin Fan
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇 SoK 试图给具身 AI 安全画张“地图”，把攻击面从“机制”里剥离出来，按信任边界分五层十二面。亮点是终于有人把“物理语义环境”和“世界状态”单独拎出来了，不再把所有 prompt 注入都混为一谈。但吐槽点也很明显：作为综述，它更多是“文献整理”而非“新发现”，58 条攻击记录在 2026 年这个时间点显得有点单薄，且大量依赖仿真评估，真实物理世界的验证比例低得可怜。作者自称“信任边界中心”，但实际还是绕不开 LLM 安全的旧套路，只是换了个具身外壳。对于防御，它指出的“下游集中”倒是实话，毕竟上游防不住，下游打补丁最省事。
This SoK attempts to draw a "map" for Embodied AI security, disentangling the attack surface from "mechanisms" and categorizing it into five layers and twelve faces based on trust boundaries. The highlight is that someone finally isolated "physical semantic environment" and "world state", no longer conflating all prompt injections. However, the criticism points are obvious: as a review, it is more "literature organization" than "new discoveries". The 58 attack records seem a bit thin at this point in 2026, and it relies heavily on simulation evaluation, with the proportion of verification in the real physical world being pitifully low. Although the authors claim to be "trust boundary-centric", they ultimately remain within the old paradigms of LLM security, merely adopting an embodied shell. Regarding defense, the observation of "downstream concentration" is accurate; since upstream defense is difficult, patching downstream is the most pragmatic solution.

研究动机Motivation

现有综述多按机制分类（如 jailbreak、后门），无法明确攻击在具身控制循环中的具体入口 [1][2]。具身系统输出直接转化为物理行为，文本层面的“合规”不等于物理安全，BadRobot 等研究已揭示语言拒绝与动作安全的错位 [9]。然而，现有分类模糊了“攻击实现方式”与“信任边界突破点”的区别，导致同一机制（如后门）在不同阶段（训练/部署）被重复统计或误判。此外，评估多停留在文本 ASR，缺乏闭环物理后果的量化 [3]。本研究旨在建立基于“首次被攻破信任边界”的 taxonomy，厘清攻击从数字输入到物理行为的传播路径，填补现有工作在跨层传播和物理可实现性评估上的空白。
Existing reviews mostly classify by mechanism (e.g., jailbreak, backdoor), failing to specify the exact entry points of attacks within the embodied control loop [1][2]. Embodied system outputs directly translate into physical behaviors; text-level "compliance" does not equal physical safety, and studies like BadRobot have revealed the misalignment between language refusal and action safety [9]. However, existing classifications blur the distinction between "attack implementation methods" and "trust boundary breach points", leading to duplicate counting or misjudgment of the same mechanism (e.g., backdoor) across different stages (training/deployment). Furthermore, evaluations mostly remain at text ASR, lacking quantification of closed-loop physical consequences [3]. This study aims to establish a taxonomy based on the "first breached trust boundary", clarifying the propagation path of attacks from digital input to physical behavior, filling the gap in existing work regarding cross-layer propagation and physical realizability assessment.

方法Method

核心贡献是提出“五层十二面”攻击面分类体系。首先定义通用闭环信息流：(u, mt, ot) → st → ht → pt → at → rt+1 → xt+1。将攻击面定义为“首次被直接操控的表示层”，而非最终后果。五层包括：开发供应链 (AS01)、外部信息输入 (AS02-AS04)、认知决策 (AS07-AS09)、基础设施协作 (AS10-AS11)、物理执行 (AS12)。创新点在于正交化“攻击面”与“攻击机制”，例如 AS05 可包含对抗补丁或传感器欺骗，AS04 区分物理语义内容与原始感知信号。通过 58 条攻击记录和 61 条防御记录编码，分析跨层传播路径（如 AS04 注入导致 AS08 规划错误）。防御建议从点式过滤转向跨层架构，强调状态溯源和运行时监控。
The core contribution is proposing a "five layers twelve faces" attack surface classification system. First, a general closed-loop information flow is defined: (u, mt, ot) → st → ht → pt → at → rt+1 → xt+1. The attack surface is defined as the "representation layer first directly manipulated", rather than the final consequence. The five layers include: Development Supply Chain (AS01), External Information Input (AS02-AS04), Cognitive Decision Making (AS07-AS09), Infrastructure Collaboration (AS10-AS11), Physical Execution (AS12). The innovation lies in orthogonalizing "attack surface" and "attack mechanism"; for instance, AS05 may encompass adversarial patches or sensor spoofing, while AS04 distinguishes physical semantic content from raw perception signals. By encoding 58 attack records and 61 defense records, cross-layer propagation paths are analyzed (e.g., AS04 injection leading to AS08 planning errors). Defense recommendations shift from point-wise filtering to cross-layer architecture, emphasizing state tracing and runtime monitoring.

实验Experiments

本研究非传统实验，而是对截至 2026 年 8 月 15 日的 58 条攻击记录和 61 条防御记录进行定量分析。数据集涵盖 A-Core（核心）、B-Adjacent（相邻）、C-System Extension（系统扩展）三级。结果显示：攻击研究集中在 AS05（多模态感知）和 AS09（动作接口），形成“感知 - 动作”双峰；防御则集中在动作级和运行时保护。AS03（上下文/记忆）、AS10（中间件）、AS11（多智能体）是稀疏区。评估指标发现现有工作过度依赖文本 ASR，缺乏轨迹偏差、碰撞等闭环物理指标。消融分析指出，仅验证“模型是否服从”会高估风险，需结合物理约束（如技能可用性、碰撞检测）。
This study is not a traditional experiment, but a quantitative analysis of 58 attack records and 61 defense records up to August 15, 2026. The dataset covers three levels: A-Core (Core), B-Adjacent (Adjacent), C-System Extension (System Extension). Results indicate that attack research concentrates on AS05 (Multimodal Perception) and AS09 (Action Interface), forming a "Perception-Action" bimodal distribution; defense concentrates on action-level and runtime protection. AS03 (Context/Memory), AS10 (Middleware), and AS11 (Multi-agent) are sparse areas. Evaluation metrics reveal that existing work overly relies on text ASR, lacking closed-loop physical metrics such as trajectory deviation and collision. Ablation analysis indicates that verifying only "whether the model obeys" will overestimate risk, necessitating combination with physical constraints (e.g., skill availability, collision detection).
