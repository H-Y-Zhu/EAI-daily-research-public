SCALE: State-Calibrated Latent Embeddings for JEPA Planning in the Right Geometry

Jiaming Hu, Yan Zheng, Tian Wang
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给JEPA世界模型来了一剂“几何矫正”的猛药。作者敏锐地指出，LeWM这类端到端模型虽然能把任务状态“解码”出来，但状态信息往往被挤在低方差方向上，导致规划器在计算欧氏距离时根本“看不见”关键信息，就像把金子藏在沙堆里，虽然金子在那儿，但铲子（规划器）只铲沙子。SCALE方法通过强制对齐潜空间距离和真实状态距离，强行把任务信息“挤”到高方差的主成分方向上，让规划器能直接“摸”到重点。这招确实有效，在五个任务上全面碾压LeWM，甚至超越了依赖预训练特征的DINO-WM。不过，这方法本质上是个“特化”的几何正则项，虽然轻量，但把任务先验（状态变量）硬塞进训练目标，算不算一种变相的“作弊”？毕竟它依赖模拟器状态，纯视觉场景下还得靠猜。但无论如何，它证明了“信息存在”不等于“几何有用”，这观点挺犀利。
This paper administers a potent dose of "geometric correction" to JEPA world models. The authors keenly point out that while end-to-end models like LeWM can "decode" task states, the state information is often squeezed into low-variance directions, causing the planner to completely "fail to see" key information when calculating Euclidean distances, just like hiding gold in a sandpile; although the gold is there, the shovel (planner) only shovels sand. The SCALE method forcibly aligns latent space distances with real state distances, squeezing task information "into" high-variance principal component directions, allowing the planner to directly "touch" the key points. This approach is indeed effective, comprehensively crushing LeWM on five tasks and even surpassing DINO-WM which relies on pre-trained features. However, this method is essentially a "specialized" geometric regularization term; although lightweight, does forcibly stuffing task priors (state variables) into the training objective count as a form of "cheating"? After all, it relies on simulator states, and in pure visual scenarios, it still has to guess. But regardless, it proves that "information existence" does not equal "geometric utility", a rather sharp viewpoint.

研究动机Motivation

JEPA（Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture）类世界模型（如LeWM [Maes et al., 2026]）通过预测潜变量进行规划，其核心在于规划器仅通过潜空间距离来评估动作序列。然而，现有研究存在一个致命盲区：任务相关状态信息是否被编码进潜空间，与这些信息是否主导了规划距离，是两码事。LeWM虽然通过SIGReg [Maes et al., 2026] 防止了表示坍塌，保证了全局各向同性，但这导致任务关键信息可能分布在低方差方向上。在欧氏距离度量下，高方差方向主导了距离计算，使得规划器对低方差方向上的关键状态变化“视而不见”。相比之下，DINO-WM [Zhou et al., 2024] 利用预训练特征，其主成分天然携带了更多任务信息，几何结构更利于规划。现有方法缺乏一种机制，能让端到端学习的表示主动将任务变异映射到规划器最敏感的几何方向上。本文旨在解决这一“可解码但不可用”的几何错位问题，证明规划性能不仅取决于信息是否存在，更取决于信息在几何空间中的分布权重。
JEPA (Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture) class world models (such as LeWM [Maes et al., 2026]) perform planning by predicting latent variables, the core of which is that the planner evaluates action sequences solely through latent space distances. However, existing research has a fatal blind spot: whether task-related state information is encoded into the latent space is one thing, and whether this information dominates the planning distance is another. Although LeWM prevents representation collapse through SIGReg [Maes et al., 2026] and ensures global isotropy, this causes task-critical information to potentially distribute in low-variance directions. Under Euclidean distance metrics, high-variance directions dominate distance calculations, making the planner "turn a blind eye" to key state changes in low-variance directions. In contrast, DINO-WM [Zhou et al., 2024] utilizes pre-trained features, where its principal components naturally carry more task information, and the geometric structure is more conducive to planning. Existing methods lack a mechanism that allows end-to-end learned representations to actively map task variations to the geometric directions most sensitive to the planner. This paper aims to solve this "decodable but unusable" geometric misalignment problem, proving that planning performance depends not only on whether information exists but more on the distribution weights of information in geometric space.

方法Method

SCALE（State-CAlibrated Latent Embeddings）提出了一种训练时的几何校准正则化，旨在重塑潜空间几何，使其与任务相关状态空间的距离分布对齐。整体流程在LeWM架构基础上，增加了状态距离对齐损失。具体而言，模型在训练时不仅进行潜变量预测（L_pred）和SIGReg防坍塌（L_sig），还引入了一个新的相关性损失L_corr。该损失函数计算采样帧对之间的潜空间欧氏距离与标准化后的任务相关状态空间欧氏距离之间的Spearman秩相关性。通过最大化这种相关性，SCALE强制要求：在真实状态空间中距离越远的样本对，其在潜空间中的距离也应越远。这种对齐不依赖具体的坐标对应，而是关注相对距离结构。此外，为了验证几何对齐的必要性，作者还设计了一个辅助控制变量（Aux），即训练一个额外的回归头来预测状态，但这不改变潜空间本身的几何结构。最终，SCALE通过单一轻量级正则项，在不增加规划时开销的情况下，将任务关键信息“推”向潜空间的高方差主成分方向，从而提升规划器对候选动作的排序能力。
SCALE (State-CAlibrated Latent Embeddings) proposes a geometric calibration regularization during training, aiming to reshape latent space geometry to align with the distance distribution of task-related state spaces. The overall process, based on the LeWM architecture, adds a state distance alignment loss. Specifically, during training, the model not only performs latent variable prediction (L_pred) and SIGReg anti-collapse (L_sig), but also introduces a new correlation loss L_corr. This loss function calculates the Spearman rank correlation between the latent space Euclidean distance between sampled frame pairs and the normalized task-related state space Euclidean distance. By maximizing this correlation, SCALE enforces that: the farther apart sample pairs are in the real state space, the farther apart they should be in latent space. This alignment does not rely on specific coordinate correspondence but focuses on relative distance structure. Furthermore, to verify the necessity of geometric alignment, the authors also designed an auxiliary control variable (Aux), which trains an additional regression head to predict states, but this does not change the geometric structure of the latent space itself. Ultimately, SCALE, through a single lightweight regularization term, without increasing planning overhead, "pushes" task-critical information towards the high-variance principal component directions of the latent space, thereby improving the planner's ranking ability for candidate actions.

实验Experiments

作者在五个具身任务（Push-T, Reacher, Cube, Two-Room, PointMaze）上进行了广泛评估，对比了LeWM、SCALE、Aux（状态回归控制）和DINO-WM。使用了三种规划求解器（CEM, iCEM, MPPI）和五个计算预算层级。结果显示，SCALE在所有15个任务-求解器组合的平均成功率上均优于LeWM，且在多个任务上超越了DINO-WM。关键发现在于，Aux虽然能实现甚至超越SCALE的状态解码能力（R2更高），但其规划性能提升远不如SCALE稳定，且未能显著改善潜空间与状态空间的距离对齐（Spearman ρ）。这直接证明了单纯提高状态的可解码性不足以提升规划，必须改变潜空间的几何结构。此外，SCALE显著提升了规划器对预测未来状态的排序一致性（Kendall τ），表明其几何校准效果在动态预测中依然有效。
The authors conducted extensive evaluations on five embodied tasks (Push-T, Reacher, Cube, Two-Room, PointMaze), comparing LeWM, SCALE, Aux (state regression control), and DINO-WM. Three planning solvers (CEM, iCEM, MPPI) and five compute budget levels were used. Results show that SCALE outperformed LeWM in the average success rate across all 15 task-solver combinations and surpassed DINO-WM in multiple tasks. The key finding is that although Aux can achieve or even exceed SCALE's state decoding capability (higher R2), its planning performance improvement is far less stable than SCALE's and failed to significantly improve the distance alignment between latent space and state space (Spearman ρ). This directly proves that simply improving state decodability is insufficient to improve planning; the geometric structure of the latent space must be changed. Additionally, SCALE significantly improved the ranking consistency of the planner for predicted future states (Kendall τ), indicating that its geometric calibration effect remains effective in dynamic prediction.
