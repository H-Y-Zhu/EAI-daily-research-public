Beyond Accuracy: Robustness, Cost, and Governance Trade-offs for Vision-Language Models in Templated Document Extraction

Kushal Patel, Pushkal Shrivastava, Mackenzie Lees, Qirui Lu, Bhargobjyoti Saikia, Liying Li
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给那些只盯着 F1 分数的 VLM 狂热者泼了盆冷水。它没搞花哨的机器人控制，而是老老实实算账：在文档提取场景下，开源模型微调后能吊打闭源 API，但成本、延迟和合规性才是生产环境的真正拦路虎。作者团队来自 John Hancock，显然懂业务痛点。虽然用了合成数据这点被吐槽，但提出的“选择框架”比单纯刷榜更有实用价值。不过，把合成数据当真理，现实中的 PII 和复杂扫描噪声可能让这套逻辑在真实世界“水土不服”，毕竟支票和机器人手臂还是两码事。这篇工作最大的贡献不是模型本身，而是把“选型”从玄学变成了数学题，尽管这数学题的变量（如 GPU 价格）可能下个月就变了，但框架本身值得借鉴。
This paper pours cold water on VLM enthusiasts who only fixate on F1 scores. It does not engage in fancy robot control, but rather does the accounting honestly: in document extraction scenarios, open-source models after fine-tuning can crush closed-source APIs, but cost, latency, and compliance are the real obstacles in production environments. The author team comes from John Hancock, clearly understanding business pain points. Although the use of synthetic data was criticized, the proposed "selection framework" has more practical value than simply ranking on leaderboards. However, treating synthetic data as truth, real-world PII and complex scan noise might make this logic "unadapted" in the real world, after all, checks and robot arms are two different things. The greatest contribution of this work is not the model itself, but turning "selection" from metaphysics into a math problem, although the variables of this math problem (such as GPU prices) might change next month, the framework itself is worth borrowing.

研究动机Motivation

现有文档 AI 评估过于关注干净基准上的准确率，忽略了实际部署的联合约束。Bai et al. (2025) 和 Marafioti et al. (2025) 提出的端到端 VLM 虽好，但缺乏对扫描缺陷、延迟预算和治理要求的考量。Chen et al. (2024) 虽涉及成本分析，但多针对纯文本。本文旨在填补这一空白，为从业者提供基于测量的选型指南，而非静态榜单。毕竟，准确率再高，如果延迟超标或无法本地部署，在金融保险等高压场景下也是零。公共榜单很少描述联合运行条件，导致从业者难以选择适合特定任务复杂度的方法，本研究通过测量驱动的方法论解决了这一决策困境。
Existing document AI evaluations focus too much on accuracy on clean benchmarks, ignoring joint constraints in actual deployment. Although the end-to-end VLMs proposed by Bai et al. (2025) and Marafioti et al. (2025) are good, they lack consideration for scan defects, latency budgets, and governance requirements. Chen et al. (2024) involves cost analysis, but mostly targets pure text. This paper aims to fill this gap, providing practitioners with a measurement-based selection guide, rather than a static leaderboard. After all, no matter how high the accuracy, if latency exceeds limits or local deployment is impossible, it is zero in high-pressure scenarios like finance and insurance. Public leaderboards rarely describe joint operating conditions, making it difficult for practitioners to select methods suitable for specific task complexities, this study solves this decision dilemma through a measurement-driven methodology.

方法Method

整体流程分为数据生成、模型评估与框架选型三阶段。首先构建 3,748 张合成支票图像，采用模板驱动管道，确保训练/测试集字段值与背景完全隔离以防泄露。其次，评估 11 个系统（含商业 API、推理模型、开源 VLM 及 OCR 基线），记录 F1、延迟及 JSON 解析成功率。核心创新在于提出一个约束优化框架：将任务复杂度（质量阈值 Qmin、延迟 Lmax、治理 Greq、体积 V）映射为推荐方案。通过最小化总拥有成本（TCO），公式 (2) 权衡开发成本与推理成本。微调策略上，Qwen 用 LoRA，SmolVLM 用全量微调，确保在固定预算下实现性能最大化。框架还引入了治理过滤器，排除不符合本地部署或数据隐私要求的模型，使得最终推荐不仅看性能，更看可行性。该框架允许从业者根据实际约束动态调整候选集，而非依赖固定排名。
The overall process is divided into three stages: data generation, model evaluation, and framework selection. First, 3,748 synthetic check images are constructed, using a template-driven pipeline to ensure training/test set field values are completely isolated from the background to prevent leakage. Second, 11 systems are evaluated (including commercial APIs, inference models, open-source VLMs, and OCR baselines), recording F1, latency, and JSON parsing success rates. The core innovation lies in proposing a constraint optimization framework: mapping task complexity (quality threshold Qmin, latency Lmax, governance Greq, volume V) to recommended solutions. By minimizing Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), formula (2) weighs development cost against inference cost. In terms of fine-tuning strategies, Qwen uses LoRA, SmolVLM uses full fine-tuning, ensuring maximum performance under a fixed budget. The framework also introduces governance filters, excluding models that do not meet local deployment or data privacy requirements, making the final recommendation look not only at performance but also at feasibility. This framework allows practitioners to dynamically adjust the candidate set based on actual constraints, rather than relying on fixed rankings.

实验Experiments

在 750 张合成支票测试集上，微调后的 Qwen2.5-VL-7B 达到 0.985 F1，超越所有零样本商业系统。但商业模型如 GPT-5 在延迟和额外字段上仍有优势。Date 字段是商业模型的普遍瓶颈，Claude Sonnet 4.5 甚至出现严重过提取。跨域测试显示，SmolVLM2-2.2B 在印度支票上保留 87% 性能，而 Qwen-3B 因训练数据年份泄露失效。成本分析表明，月处理量低于 44 万份时 API 更优，高于此阈值则自托管更划算。保险表单任务中，开源模型在 Checkbox 字段表现显著优于 GPT-4.1。此外，GPU 敏感性测试显示 H100 比 A100 快 1.4-1.7 倍，但不影响相对排名。这些结果揭示了单一准确率指标的局限性，强调了多目标优化的必要性。
On a test set of 750 synthetic checks, the fine-tuned Qwen2.5-VL-7B reached 0.985 F1, surpassing all zero-shot commercial systems. But commercial models like GPT-5 still have advantages in latency and extra fields. The Date field is a common bottleneck for commercial models, with Claude Sonnet 4.5 even exhibiting severe over-extraction. Cross-domain tests show that SmolVLM2-2.2B retained 87% performance on Indian checks, while Qwen-3B failed due to training data year leakage. Cost analysis indicates that when monthly volume is below 440,000, APIs are better, and above this threshold, self-hosting is more cost-effective. In insurance form tasks, open-source models performed significantly better than GPT-4.1 on Checkbox fields. Additionally, GPU sensitivity tests show H100 is 1.4-1.7 times faster than A100, but does not affect relative ranking. These results reveal the limitations of single accuracy metrics, emphasizing the necessity of multi-objective optimization.
