ResSafe: Learning Safety Filtering with Residual Reinforcement Learning for Humanoids

Gechen Qu, Tong Zhang, Bike Zhang, Yen-Jen Wang, Koushil Sreenath, Claire Tomlin
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给足平衡的人形机器人加了个“安全保镖”，思路是“术业有专攻”：让主策略负责干活，残差策略负责保命。虽然“性能 - 安全解耦”听起来很性感，能避开单策略训练中的帕累托陷阱，但本质上还是靠数据驱动的黑盒学习，缺乏传统安全控制理论的硬保证。作者声称残差策略像最小范数安全滤波器，但实验显示它比判别超平面滤波器更保守，这到底是鲁棒性还是过拟合？硬件实验虽然展示了落地能力，但没提感知，纯靠本体感觉平衡，离人类那种“眼观六路”的平衡还有距离。总的来说，是个实用的工程改进，但理论深度略显不足，别指望它能像 CBF 那样给出数学上的安全证明。此外，残差策略的保守性可能限制了性能上限，且无法提供硬安全保证，这在极端场景下仍是隐患。
This paper adds a "safety bodyguard" to humanoid robots that are already balanced, with the idea of "specialization": letting the main policy handle tasks and the residual policy handle survival. Although "performance-safety decoupling" sounds appealing, avoiding the Pareto trap in single-policy training, it essentially relies on data-driven black-box learning, lacking rigorous guarantees from traditional safety control theory. The authors claim the residual policy acts like a minimum norm safety filter, but experiments show it is more conservative than the discriminant hyperplane filter; is this robustness or overfitting? Although hardware experiments demonstrate deployment capability, perception is not mentioned; it relies purely on proprioception for balance, still far from human-like "comprehensive situational awareness" balance. Overall, it is a practical engineering improvement, but theoretical depth is slightly insufficient; do not expect it to provide mathematical safety proofs like CBF. Additionally, the conservatism of the residual policy may limit the performance ceiling and cannot provide hard safety guarantees, which remains a potential risk in extreme scenarios.

研究动机Motivation

人形机器人控制面临高维动力学和接触交互的复杂性，强化学习（RL）虽能实现运动，但容易生成危险动作导致摔倒 [1, 2]。现有方法试图在单策略中平衡性能与安全，但这本质是多目标优化，梯度下降往往收敛到次优帕累托前沿 [1]。基于约束 MDP 的安全 RL 方法 [3, 4] 难以捕捉瞬时约束违反（如碰撞）。传统安全滤波器依赖显式动力学模型或控制障碍函数（CBF）[5]，在高维系统中扩展性差。此外，基于 Hamilton-Jacobi 可达性的方法 [8] 需要复杂的 Bellman 方程。因此，如何在不牺牲性能的前提下，学习一个可泛化的安全过滤机制，成为关键痛点。现有的安全 RL 往往通过惩罚项耦合目标，导致性能下降，而模型依赖方法又难以适应复杂动力学，亟需一种解耦且数据驱动的方案。
Humanoid robot control faces the complexity of high-dimensional dynamics and contact interactions; although Reinforcement Learning (RL) can achieve locomotion, it easily generates dangerous actions leading to falls [1, 2]. Existing methods attempt to balance performance and safety in a single policy, but this is essentially multi-objective optimization, where gradient descent often converges to a suboptimal Pareto frontier [1]. Safety RL methods based on constrained MDPs [3, 4] struggle to capture instantaneous constraint violations (e.g., collisions). Traditional safety filters rely on explicit dynamic models or Control Barrier Functions (CBF) [5], which have poor scalability in high-dimensional systems. Additionally, methods based on Hamilton-Jacobi reachability [8] require complex Bellman equations. Therefore, how to learn a generalizable safety filtering mechanism without sacrificing performance becomes a key pain point. Existing safety RL often couples objectives through penalty terms, leading to performance degradation, while model-dependent methods struggle to adapt to complex dynamics, urgently needing a decoupled and data-driven solution.

方法Method

核心框架 ResSafe 将控制解耦为两部分：参考策略（Nominal Policy）负责任务性能，残差策略（Residual Policy）负责安全修正。
1. **参考策略训练**：基于平衡运动追踪管道 [2]，生成 10,000 种多样化运动数据集，覆盖不同难度。
2. **残差策略学习**：采用 PPO 算法，输入为状态和参考策略动作。奖励函数设计为生存时间最大化（模拟可达性）加上最小范数惩罚（模仿安全滤波器修正），并加入基座速度稳定和动作平滑项。
3. **训练策略**：引入随机负载和推搡扰动以暴露失败状态；使用多个参考策略检查点训练残差策略，增强泛化能力。
4. **执行**：最终动作 = 参考动作 + 残差修正。该方法隐式学习了安全过滤机制，无需在线优化，类似最小范数安全滤波器 [6] 的结构，但通过数据驱动避免了显式模型依赖。
5. **网络结构**：采用不对称 Actor-Critic，Critic 可访问真值状态，Actor 仅用部署时观测。
6. **状态空间**：包含本体感知状态、参考动作及历史状态，维度约 122。
通过这种方式，残差策略充当了隐式安全过滤器，在保持性能的同时修正潜在危险动作。
The core framework ResSafe decouples control into two parts: Nominal Policy responsible for task performance, Residual Policy responsible for safety correction.
1. **Reference Policy Training**: Based on the balance motion tracking pipeline [2], generate 10,000 diverse motion datasets covering different difficulties.
2. **Residual Policy Learning**: Adopt PPO algorithm, input is state and reference policy actions. Reward function designed as survival time maximization (simulating reachability) plus minimum norm penalty (imitating safety filter correction), and add base velocity stability and action smoothness terms.
3. **Training Strategy**: Introduce random loads and pushing perturbations to expose failure states; use multiple reference policy checkpoints to train the residual policy, enhancing generalization capability.
4. **Execution**: Final action = Reference action + Residual correction. This method implicitly learns a safety filtering mechanism, requiring no online optimization, similar in structure to minimum norm safety filter [6], but avoids explicit model dependence through data-driven means.
5. **Network Structure**: Adopt asymmetric Actor-Critic, Critic can access ground truth state, Actor only uses deployment-time observations.
6. **State Space**: Includes proprioceptive states, reference actions, and historical states, dimension approximately 122.
In this way, the residual policy acts as an implicit safety filter, correcting potential dangerous actions while maintaining performance.

实验Experiments

在 Unitree G1 人形机器人上验证，包含仿真（IsaacGym）和真机实验。
**基线**：无随机化参考策略、带负载随机化参考策略、判别超平面（DH）安全滤波器。
**指标**：跟踪误差（Epos-l）和摔倒率（Fall Rate）。
**结果**：ResSafe 在负载扰动下摔倒率最低（1.11%），且跟踪误差优于带安全惩罚的基线。多检查点训练显著提升了跨参考策略的泛化能力，单检查点训练则泛化差。真机实验显示，ResSafe 在多种负载和运动下均优于基线和 DH 滤波器。
**发现**：解耦学习实现了更好的性能 - 安全 - 鲁棒性权衡，但残差策略比 DH 滤波器更保守。消融实验表明，随机负载和推搡扰动对提升鲁棒性至关重要，缺少这些扰动会导致策略过于保守。
Validated on Unitree G1 humanoid robot, including simulation (IsaacGym) and real-robot experiments.
**Baseline**: No randomization reference policy, Load randomization reference policy, Discriminant Hyperplane (DH) safety filter.
**Metrics**: Tracking error (Epos-l) and Fall Rate.
**Results**: ResSafe has the lowest fall rate under load perturbations (1.11%), and tracking error is better than the baseline with safety penalties. Multi-checkpoint training significantly improved generalization across reference policies, while single-checkpoint training had poor generalization. Real-robot experiments show ResSafe outperforms baselines and DH filter under various loads and motions.
**Findings**: Decoupled learning achieves better performance-safety-robustness trade-off, but residual policy is more conservative than DH filter. Ablation studies show random loads and pushing perturbations are crucial for improving robustness; lacking these perturbations leads to overly conservative policies.
