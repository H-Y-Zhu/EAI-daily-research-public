Early to Share, Late to Save: Synchronisation-Driven Communication Gating in Bandwidth-Constrained Cooperative VLN

Arav Gupta, Nivedan Yakolli, Avinash Gautam
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给带宽受限的协作导航泼了盆冷水。大家以为机器人沟通得像人类一样“不懂就问”，结果作者发现，在带宽紧巴巴的情况下，聪明的做法是“早说早享受，晚说没机会”。他们搞了个“后见之明门控”（Hindsight Gating），不用高方差的 REINFORCE，而是用监督学习直接贴标签。虽然 SR 在未见场景下提升有限，甚至略低于单智能体基线，但隐藏状态对齐效果杠杠的。最反直觉的是，门控在置信度高时反而更爱发射，而不是不确定时。不过，这方法依赖不对称路径设定，两个机器人一个跑全程一个跑半程，现实里真这么安排吗？而且没提多智能体扩展，感觉像是为了刷指标而设计的“特供版”协作。
This paper puts a damper on bandwidth-constrained collaborative navigation. Everyone assumed robots should communicate like humans "ask if you don't understand", but the authors found that under tight bandwidth constraints, the smart approach is "speak early to enjoy, speak late to miss out". They developed a "Hindsight Gating", avoiding high-variance REINFORCE and instead using supervised learning for direct labeling. Although SR improvement is limited in unseen scenarios, even slightly lower than the single-agent baseline, the hidden state alignment effect is excellent. Most counter-intuitively, the gating prefers to emit when confidence is high, rather than when uncertain. However, this method relies on an asymmetric path setting, where one robot runs the full path and the other runs half, questioning if this is truly arranged in reality? Moreover, it does not mention multi-agent extension, feeling like a "special version" collaboration designed just to boost metrics.

研究动机Motivation

现有协作 VLN 方法大多假设通信无限，忽略了现实中的带宽限制 [10, 16]。比如 Co-NavGPT [16] 和 CAMON [10] 都假设无约束通信。而像 IC3Net [14] 虽然引入了门控，但使用 REINFORCE 训练，因果链太长导致方差大，训练不稳定。本文想解决的是：在预算有限时，什么时候该说话？这很重要，因为无线电、隐私都限制通信。现有方法没解决“何时通信”的问题，只关注“说什么”或“压缩”。Farooq et al. [6] 研究消息压缩而非时机。本文填补了带宽受限下的通信时机空白，并提出了更稳定的训练范式。
Existing collaborative VLN methods mostly assume infinite communication, ignoring real-world bandwidth limits [10, 16]. For example Co-NavGPT [16] and CAMON [10] both assume unconstrained communication. While IC3Net [14] introduces gating, it uses REINFORCE training, where the long causal chain leads to high variance and unstable training. This paper aims to solve: when to speak under limited budget? This is important because radio and privacy both limit communication. Existing methods do not solve the "when to communicate" problem, focusing only on "what to say" or "compression". Farooq et al. [6] study message compression rather than timing. This paper fills the gap in communication timing under bandwidth constraints and proposes a more stable training paradigm.

方法Method

整体分三阶段。先单智能体预训练（模仿学习）。然后收集“后见之明”标签：如果 A 错了但 B 对了，标记为需要通信。用 BCE 训练门控网络，输入是隐藏状态和剩余预算。最后联合微调。创新点在于用监督学习替代强化学习，避免方差。门控只输入隐藏状态，不显式输入不确定性，让模型自己学。具体流程是：1. 预训练导航策略；2. 无通信运行，标记错误步骤（伙伴正确）；3. 训练 MLP 门控；4. 冻结门控微调策略。路径分配上，Agent 0 走全程，Agent 1 走后半程，形成信息不对称。损失函数是 BCE，输入包含剩余预算 brem(t) 以调节发射率。导航骨干使用 CLIP ViT-B/32 编码，GRU 维护隐藏状态，CrossModalAttention 生成上下文向量。门控网络是 3 层 MLP，仅接收 hi(t) 和 brem(t)。
Overall divided into three stages. First, single-agent pretraining (imitation learning). Then collect "hindsight" labels: if A is wrong but B is right, mark as requiring communication. Train the gating network using BCE, with inputs being the hidden state and remaining budget. Finally, joint fine-tuning. The innovation lies in replacing reinforcement learning with supervised learning to avoid variance. The gating only inputs the hidden state, not explicitly inputting uncertainty, letting the model learn it itself. The specific process is: 1. Pretrain navigation policy; 2. Run without communication, mark error steps (partner correct); 3. Train MLP gating; 4. Freeze gating and fine-tune policy. In path allocation, Agent 0 walks the full path, Agent 1 walks the latter half, forming information asymmetry. The loss function is BCE, with inputs including the remaining budget brem(t) to adjust the emission rate. The navigation backbone uses CLIP ViT-B/32 encoding, GRU maintains the hidden state, and CrossModalAttention generates the context vector. The gating network is a 3-layer MLP, receiving only hi(t) and brem(t).

实验Experiments

用 R2R [1] 和 Matterport3D [2]。指标是 SR 和 SPL。基线有无通信、全通信、随机门控、熵门控。结果发现门控在早期步骤（0-2）发射率高，且置信度高时发射。隐藏状态对齐增益 +0.072，比随机高 260%。预算 B=3 时接近全通信。val-unseen 下 SR 提升不明显，但对齐显著。证明了早期同步优于后期纠错。Table 1 显示 B=3 时 SR 8.9%，接近全通信。Table 3 显示对齐增益远超随机和熵基线。统计显著性检验确认了早期步骤差异显著。
Used R2R [1] and Matterport3D [2]. Metrics are SR and SPL. Baselines include no communication, full communication, random gating, and entropy gating. Results found the gating has high emission rates in early steps (0-2), and emits when confidence is high. Hidden state alignment gain is +0.072, 260% higher than random. At budget B=3, it is close to full communication. Under val-unseen, SR improvement is not obvious, but alignment is significant. This proves early synchronization is superior to late correction. Statistical significance tests confirmed the difference in early steps is significant.
