Co-VLA: Consensus-based Federated Training for Vision-Language-Action Models

Haolong Li, Guner Dilsad Er, Michael Muehlebach, Joerg Stueckler
abspdf

论文点评Review

Co-VLA 给联邦学习圈泼了一盆冷水，但也端上了一碗热汤。它没搞花里胡哨的 MoE 路由，而是老老实实用 ADMM 搞“共识”，强行让各怀鬼胎的机器人客户端“统一口径”。这招在 LoRA 微调上特别灵，解决了传统 FedAvg 在异构数据下“各练各的、最后乱成一锅粥”的顽疾。不过，别高兴太早，这方法收敛速度还是比集中式训练慢，而且作者也没敢吹嘘隐私保护有多强——毕竟模型参数泄露数据这事儿，ADMM 也救不了。总的来说，这是个“虽然慢但很稳”的实用主义方案，适合那些数据分散但又不想被隐私墙挡住的机器人团队。
Co-VLA poured a bucket of cold water on the federated learning community, yet also served a bowl of hot soup. It did not employ fancy MoE routing, but rather straightforwardly used ADMM to establish "consensus", forcibly aligning the "discourse" of robot clients with divergent interests. This approach proves particularly effective for LoRA fine-tuning, resolving the chronic issue of traditional FedAvg where clients "train independently, resulting in chaos" under heterogeneous data. However, do not celebrate too soon; the convergence speed of this method remains slower than centralized training, and the authors did not dare to exaggerate the strength of privacy protection—after all, regarding the leakage of data through model parameters, ADMM cannot rescue the situation. Overall, this is a pragmatic solution that is "slow but stable", suitable for robot teams with distributed data who do not wish to be hindered by privacy walls.

研究动机Motivation

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) 模型在机器人领域表现亮眼，但数据收集成本高且分散在不同实验室、任务和地点，集中式训练既不现实也不合规 [1]-[4]。联邦学习虽能解决数据孤岛问题，但在处理非独立同分布（non-i.i.d.）数据时，简单的参数平均（如 FedAvg）会导致模型向各客户端的局部最优漂移，严重损害全局性能 [9]。特别是在 LoRA 微调场景下，直接平均低秩因子会引入严重的双线性聚合不匹配问题，导致性能下降 [10]-[12]。现有工作如 FedVLA 依赖特定的 MoE 架构，缺乏通用性 [15]。因此，亟需一种既能处理异构数据，又能统一适配全模型训练和参数高效微调（如 LoRA/SoRA）的联邦优化框架。
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models have shown promising performance in the robotics domain; however, data collection is costly and distributed across different laboratories, tasks, and locations, making centralized training neither practical nor compliant [1]-[4]. Although federated learning can address data silo issues, when handling non-independent and identically distributed (non-i.i.d.) data, simple parameter averaging (such as FedAvg) causes the model to drift towards local optima of individual clients, severely compromising global performance [9]. Particularly in LoRA fine-tuning scenarios, directly averaging low-rank factors introduces severe bilinear aggregation mismatch problems, leading to performance degradation [10]-[12]. Existing works such as FedVLA rely on specific MoE architectures, lacking generality [15]. Therefore, there is an urgent need for a federated optimization framework capable of both handling heterogeneous data and uniformly adapting to full model training and parameter-efficient fine-tuning (such as LoRA/SoRA).

方法Method

Co-VLA 的核心是将联邦 VLA 训练建模为全局共识优化问题，利用交替方向乘子法（ADMM）替代传统的参数平均。整体流程分为三步：1. 本地更新：每个客户端 $i$ 在本地数据 $D_i$ 上优化局部模型 $x_i$，目标函数包含原始损失项和一项二次正则项，强制 $x_i$ 向全局共识变量 $z$ 靠拢；2. 对偶更新：客户端计算拉格朗日乘子 $u_i$ 的增量，累积与全局模型的偏差；3. 全局聚合：服务器计算所有客户端的 $x_i + u_i$ 的平均值作为新的全局共识变量 $z$，并广播回客户端。创新点在于：对于全模型训练，直接约束参数一致；对于 LoRA 微调，将共识约束直接施加在低秩因子 $A$ 和 $B$ 上，避免了 $E[B_i A_i]
eq E[B_i]E[A_i]$ 的聚合误差；对于稀疏 LoRA (SoRA)，将门控参数 $g$ 纳入共识变量，并在服务器端通过软阈值操作诱导稀疏性，实现自适应秩选择。该方法模型无关，且支持过松弛加速收敛。
The core of Co-VLA lies in modeling federated VLA training as a global consensus optimization problem, utilizing the Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM) to replace traditional parameter averaging. The overall process is divided into three steps: 1. Local Update: Each client $i$ optimizes the local model $x_i$ on local data $D_i$; the objective function includes the original loss term and a quadratic regularization term, forcing $x_i$ to converge towards the global consensus variable $z$; 2. Dual Update: Clients calculate the increment of the Lagrange multiplier $u_i$, accumulating the deviation from the global model; 3. Global Aggregation: The server calculates the average of all clients' $x_i + u_i$ as the new global consensus variable $z$ and broadcasts it back to the clients. The innovations lie in: for full model training, directly constraining parameter consistency; for LoRA fine-tuning, applying the consensus constraint directly onto the low-rank factors $A$ and $B$, avoiding aggregation errors where $E[B_i A_i]
eq E[B_i]E[A_i]$; for Sparse LoRA (SoRA), incorporating gating parameters $g$ into the consensus variable and inducing sparsity via soft-thresholding operations on the server side to achieve adaptive rank selection. This method is model-agnostic and supports over-relaxation to accelerate convergence.

实验Experiments

实验在 LIBERO 仿真基准和 Open X-Embodiment 真实世界数据集上进行，对比模型包括 SmolVLA 和 X-VLA。基线涵盖 FedAvg、DiLoCo 以及针对 LoRA 的 FLoRA、FlexLoRA。评测指标为任务成功率（LIBERO）和动作预测误差（真实世界）。结果显示：在 LIBERO 全模型训练中，Co-VLA 性能接近集中式训练，优于 FedAvg 和 DiLoCo；在 LoRA 微调中，Co-VLA 显著优于 FLoRA 和 FlexLoRA，且收敛更快。真实世界实验中，Co-VLA 在混合异构数据源下取得了最小的平均误差，并在高难度的堆叠任务中成功率最高。消融实验表明，SoRA 变体能以 55%-60% 的参数量维持相近性能。
Experiments were conducted on the LIBERO simulation benchmark and the Open X-Embodiment real-world dataset, with comparative models including SmolVLA and X-VLA. Baselines covered FedAvg, DiLoCo, as well as FLoRA and FlexLoRA for LoRA. Evaluation metrics included task success rate (LIBERO) and action prediction error (real-world). Results indicate: in LIBERO full model training, Co-VLA performance approached centralized training and outperformed FedAvg and DiLoCo; in LoRA fine-tuning, Co-VLA significantly outperformed FLoRA and FlexLoRA, with faster convergence. In real-world experiments, Co-VLA achieved the minimum average error under mixed heterogeneous data sources and the highest success rate in high-difficulty stacking tasks. Ablation studies indicate that the SoRA variant maintains comparable performance with only 55%-60% of the parameter count.
