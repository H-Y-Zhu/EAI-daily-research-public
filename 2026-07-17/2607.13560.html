Grounded world models in biological organisms and future embodied AI

Giovanni Pezzulo, Davide Nuzzi, Marco D'Alessandro, Riccardo Proietti, Roberto Bottini, Paul Cisek
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文像是一盆冷水，泼向了正沉浸在“大语言模型万能论”狂欢中的具身AI圈。作者犀利地指出，当前的VLA模型虽然热闹，但本质上是“倒置”的：它们试图用语言作为脚手架去挂载感知，而生物界恰恰相反，是用身体与环境的互动构建了世界模型，语言只是后来者。文章没有堆砌新代码，而是通过五个神经回路案例（如海马体导航、前馈抑制等）进行了一场“降维打击”，痛陈当前AI缺乏内驱力、被动学习且不懂“自我”的致命缺陷。虽然它没给出一个能直接跑通的“生物AI”算法，但其对“被动训练”范式的批判和对“具身性”本质的回归，堪称对当前AI界“空中楼阁”式发展的当头棒喝。
This paper is like a bucket of cold water poured upon the embodied AI community, which is immersed in the carnival of the "omnipotence of large language models". The author sharply points out that although current VLA models are bustling, they are essentially "inverted": they attempt to use language as scaffolding to mount perception, whereas the biological world is exactly the opposite, constructing world models through the interaction of body and environment, with language merely being a later addition. The article does not pile up new code, but conducts a "dimensionality reduction strike" through five neural circuit cases (such as hippocampus navigation, feedforward inhibition, etc.), painfully exposing the fatal flaws of current AI lacking intrinsic drive, passive learning, and failing to understand "self". Although it does not provide a "biological AI" algorithm that can run directly, its critique of the "passive training" paradigm and return to the essence of "embodiment" can be regarded as a wake-up blow to the "castle in the air" development of the current AI community.

研究动机Motivation

研究旨在解决当前具身AI（Embodied AI）中世界模型（World Models）构建的根本性错位问题。现有主流方法（如VLA模型）主要依赖大规模被动训练，以语言数据为脚手架，将其他模态信息挂载其上 [1-7]。这种范式存在严重不足：首先，它缺乏生物智能中那种通过主动交互获得的“接地”（grounded）知识，导致AI难以真正理解物理和社会现实的动态结构；其次，现有系统缺乏内驱力，无法像生物那样进行开放式的持续学习 [11]；最后，语言在生物中是建立在感知运动经验之上的，而当前AI却试图用语言去解释世界，导致“意义”缺失。作者引用Parr等人关于主动推断的框架 [8]，指出生物智能是通过预测误差最小化来构建模型的，而当前AI的被动学习无法复现这一过程，限制了其在复杂现实环境中的泛化和推理能力。
The research aims to solve the fundamental misalignment problem in the construction of World Models in current Embodied AI. Existing mainstream methods (such as VLA models) mainly rely on large-scale passive training, using language data as scaffolding to mount other modality information on it [1-7]. This paradigm has serious deficiencies: first, it lacks the "grounded" knowledge obtained through active interaction in biological intelligence, leading to AI's difficulty in truly understanding the dynamic structure of physical and social reality; second, existing systems lack intrinsic drive and cannot perform open-ended continuous learning like biological organisms [11]; finally, language in biology is built upon perceptual-motor experience, while current AI attempts to use language to explain the world, leading to a lack of "meaning". The author cites Parr et al.'s framework on active inference [8], pointing out that biological intelligence constructs models through minimizing prediction error, while current AI's passive learning cannot reproduce this process, limiting its generalization and reasoning capabilities in complex real-world environments.

方法Method

本文并未提出新的神经网络架构或训练算法，而是采用了一种“逆向工程”式的理论分析方法，通过解构生物神经回路来提炼具身AI的设计原则。整体逻辑框架是：首先定义“接地世界模型”的核心特征（即通过主动交互将内部动力学与外部世界对齐），然后选取五个关键的生物神经回路案例进行深度剖析，最后归纳出指导未来AI设计的通用原则。具体模块包括：1. 导航回路分析：以海马体-内嗅皮层系统为例，展示生物如何利用内禀动力学（如网格细胞）构建物理及概念空间的认知地图，并指出其通过主动运动校准的机制 [14, 15]。2. affordance（可供性）感知：分析背侧/腹侧视觉流如何协同工作，将感知直接转化为行动可能性的预测与选择，而非静态物体识别 [48, 49]。3. 主动感知与探索：阐述神经调节系统（如去甲肾上腺素）如何作为“元世界模型”调节探索与利用的平衡，驱动内驱力学习 [60, 61]。4. 稳态控制与情绪：解析内脏感觉系统如何通过预测内部状态来驱动行为，确立生物体的自主性和价值评估机制 [63-65]。5. 自我-世界区分：介绍前馈抑制（corollary discharge）机制如何区分自身动作与环境反馈，构建“自我”概念 [76-78]。最终，将这些生物机制抽象为AI设计的五大原则：内禀动力学基础、行动中心主义、主动开放学习、早期预测机制支撑高阶认知、以及社会共享模型的构建。
This paper does not propose new neural network architectures or training algorithms, but adopts a "reverse engineering" theoretical analysis method, extracting design principles for embodied AI by deconstructing biological neural circuits. The overall logical framework is: first define the core characteristics of "grounded world models" (i.e., aligning internal dynamics with the external world through active interaction), then select five key biological neural circuit cases for in-depth analysis, and finally summarize general principles guiding future AI design. Specific modules include: 1. Navigation circuit analysis: Taking the hippocampus-entorhinal cortex system as an example, showing how biological organisms use intrinsic dynamics (such as grid cells) to construct cognitive maps of physical and conceptual spaces, and pointing out its mechanism of calibration through active movement [14, 15]. 2. Affordance (affordance) perception: Analyzing how dorsal/ventral visual streams work together to directly transform perception into prediction and selection of action possibilities, rather than static object recognition [48, 49]. 3. Active perception and exploration: Explaining how neuromodulatory systems (such as norepinephrine) act as "meta-world models" to regulate the balance between exploration and exploitation, driving intrinsic drive learning [60, 61]. 4. Homeostatic control and emotion: Elucidating how the visceral sensory system drives behavior by predicting internal states, establishing the autonomy and value assessment mechanisms of biological organisms [63-65]. 5. Self-world distinction: Introducing how the feedforward inhibition (corollary discharge) mechanism distinguishes between self-actions and environmental feedback to construct the concept of "self" [76-78]. Finally, abstract these biological mechanisms into five major principles for AI design: intrinsic dynamics foundation, action-centrism, active open learning, early prediction mechanisms supporting higher-order cognition, and the construction of social shared models.

实验Experiments

本文属于理论综述与观点阐述类研究，未包含传统的定量实验、数据集训练或基线对比。文中引用的“实验”证据主要来源于神经科学领域的经典文献，例如引用Kato等人关于线虫全脑神经动力学的钙成像数据 [22]，以及关于大鼠、猴子和人类在空间、概念、价值和社会空间中导航的fMRI/电生理研究 [32-36]。这些被引用的研究展示了生物神经回路在物理和抽象空间中的活动模式（如网格细胞的六重调制、海马体的重放现象等），作为论证“接地世界模型”存在的实证基础。文章没有进行消融实验，而是通过逻辑推演和跨物种比较，论证了当前AI范式与生物机制的差距。主要发现是：生物智能的涌现依赖于主动交互和内部动力学，而当前AI的被动语言中心范式无法自然复现这一过程，除非引入类似的主动学习和社会交互机制。
This paper is a theoretical review and opinion piece, not containing traditional quantitative experiments, dataset training, or baseline comparisons. The "experimental" evidence cited in the text mainly comes from classic literature in the field of neuroscience, such as citing Kato et al.'s calcium imaging data on nematode whole-brain neural dynamics [22], and fMRI/electrophysiological studies on navigation in spatial, conceptual, value, and social spaces by rats, monkeys, and humans [32-36]. These cited studies show the activity patterns of biological neural circuits in physical and abstract spaces (such as the six-fold modulation of grid cells, hippocampal replay phenomena, etc.), serving as empirical evidence for the existence of "grounded world models". The article did not conduct ablation studies, but demonstrated the gap between current AI paradigms and biological mechanisms through logical deduction and cross-species comparison. The main findings are: the emergence of biological intelligence relies on active interaction and internal dynamics, while the current AI passive language-centric paradigm cannot naturally reproduce this process, unless similar active learning and social interaction mechanisms are introduced.
