🧭 Force Action Policy Model

· 发展脉络· Development

Force Action Policy Model 旨在解决机器人在接触丰富（Contact-rich）环境中执行任务时，因缺乏力觉反馈而导致的柔顺控制与物理交互能力不足问题。该方向从早期的阻抗控制与力位混合控制，演进为利用扩散模型（Diffusion Policy）和视觉-触觉-语言模型（VLA）进行端到端策略学习。近期发展聚焦于构建触觉原生的世界模型（World-Action Models），通过融合多模态感知（视觉、触觉、力觉）与语义理解，实现从仿真到现实的无缝迁移及复杂物理参数的在线辨识。Force Action Policy Model aims to address the insufficiency in compliant control and physical interaction capabilities of robots executing tasks in contact-rich environments due to the lack of force feedback. This direction has evolved from early impedance control and force-position hybrid control to end-to-end policy learning utilizing Diffusion Policy and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Recent developments focus on constructing tactile-native world models (World-Action Models), achieving seamless sim-to-real transfer and online identification of complex physical parameters by fusing multi-modal perception (vision, touch, force) with semantic understanding.

Force-Aware VLA & World ModelsForce-Aware VLA & World Models

Force-Aware VLA 与世界模型旨在解决纯视觉具身智能在接触任务中缺乏物理反馈的痛点，将力/触觉提升为与视觉、语言同等的一等模态。演进路径从早期的简单模态拼接（如 ForceVLA 引入力感知 MoE），发展到解决多模态冲突的架构创新（如 OmniVTLA 的语义对齐、Tactile-WAM 的不对称注意力隔离触觉污染），再到构建触觉参与的世界模型（VT-WAM 利用 Flow Matching 融合稀疏触觉）。核心思路是通过动态融合或物理隔离机制，让模型在预测动作或世界状态时显式利用接触力信息，从而显著提升插拔、组装等接触丰富任务的泛化性与鲁棒性。Force-Aware VLA and World Models aim to address the pain point of purely visual embodied intelligence lacking physical feedback in contact tasks, elevating force/touch to an equal modality alongside vision and language. The evolutionary path has progressed from early simple modality concatenation (e.g., ForceVLA introducing force-aware MoE) to architectural innovations addressing multimodal conflicts (e.g., OmniVTLA's semantic alignment, Tactile-WAM's asymmetric attention isolating tactile pollution), and further to constructing tactile-participating world models (VT-WAM utilizing Flow Matching to fuse sparse tactile data). The core idea is to enable models to explicitly utilize contact force information when predicting actions or world states through dynamic fusion or physical isolation mechanisms, thereby significantly improving the generalization and robustness of contact-rich tasks such as insertion and assembly.
