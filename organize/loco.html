🧭 Locomotion / Humanoid

· 发展脉络· Development

该方向聚焦于人形机器人的全身运动与操作（Loco-Manipulation），核心挑战在于如何统一下肢行走与上肢操作，并实现从仿真到现实的无缝迁移。发展脉络从早期的运动跟踪与遥操作，逐步演进为基于世界模型（World Models）和扩散策略（Diffusion Policies）的通用控制架构。近期研究重点转向解决动力学失配、力控精度以及利用大规模人类演示数据训练通用基础模型，以实现复杂环境下的敏捷交互。This direction focuses on whole-body motion and manipulation (Loco-Manipulation) of humanoid robots, where the core challenge lies in unifying lower-limb locomotion with upper-limb manipulation and achieving seamless sim-to-real transfer. The development trajectory has evolved from early motion tracking and teleoperation to general control architectures based on World Models and Diffusion Policies. Recent research priorities have shifted towards addressing dynamics mismatch, force control accuracy, and leveraging large-scale human demonstration data to train general foundation models, enabling agile interaction in complex environments.

全身遥操作与低成本数据采集Whole-body teleoperation and low-cost data acquisition

该方向旨在打破人形机器人全身遥操作对昂贵动捕系统的依赖，通过单目视觉、VR 或低成本传感器实现低成本数据采集与实时控制。演进路径从早期引入轮式底盘的混合遥操作（Mobile ALOHA），发展到纯视觉驱动的姿态估计与仿真清洗（H2O），再到引入压力传感增强物理一致性（PressMimic）。核心思路是利用多模态融合与数据蒸馏技术，在低成本硬件约束下解决全身动作的实时性、泛化性与物理真实性问题，逐步从特定任务演示向通用全身控制逼近。This direction aims to break the dependence of humanoid robot whole-body teleoperation on expensive motion capture systems, achieving low-cost data acquisition and real-time control through monocular vision, VR, or low-cost sensors. The evolutionary path has progressed from early hybrid teleoperation with wheeled chassis (Mobile ALOHA), to vision-driven pose estimation and simulation cleaning (H2O), and further to pressure sensing enhancement for physical consistency (PressMimic). The core approach utilizes multimodal fusion and data distillation techniques to address real-time performance, generalization, and physical authenticity of whole-body actions under low-cost hardware constraints, gradually moving from specific task demonstrations towards general whole-body control.
