🧭 Robot assemblyRobot assembly

· 发展脉络· Development

机器人装配领域正从传统的基于几何规则的精确控制，向融合触觉感知、多模态大模型与强化学习的通用灵巧操作演进。早期研究聚焦于解决几何变化下的视觉仿真到现实迁移及高精度插入基准测试，随后引入触觉扩散策略与力控恢复机制以应对接触丰富的复杂场景。最新进展则致力于构建基础模型与长视界视觉-语言-动作（VLA）策略，通过合成数据驱动和预训练实现家具组装等复杂任务的泛化与自动化。The field of robotic assembly is evolving from traditional geometry-rule-based precise control towards general dexterous manipulation that integrates tactile perception, multimodal large models, and reinforcement learning. Early research focused on addressing visual simulation-to-reality transfer under geometric variations and high-precision insertion benchmarks, subsequently introducing tactile diffusion strategies and force-control recovery mechanisms to cope with contact-rich complex scenarios. Latest advances are dedicated to building foundation models and long-horizon vision-language-action (VLA) policies, achieving generalization and automation for complex tasks such as furniture assembly through synthetic data-driven approaches and pre-training.

触觉与力域策略Tactile and Force-Domain Strategies

该方向聚焦于利用触觉感知与力域策略解决精密装配中的接触不确定性难题。核心思路是从传统的运动域控制转向力/力矩域的直接建模，利用扩散模型生成高维力分布以应对复杂接触动力学。演进路径上，早期工作多依赖刚性接触模型或浅层力控，近期研究如TacDiffusion则首次将扩散策略引入力域，通过生成式模型处理多模态触觉反馈，实现了从运动指令到力矩分布的端到端映射，显著提升了零样本迁移能力与接触稳定性，标志着装配控制从“位置跟随”向“力场规划”的范式转变。This direction focuses on utilizing tactile perception and force-domain strategies to resolve contact uncertainty challenges in precision assembly. The core approach involves shifting from traditional motion-domain control to direct modeling in the force/torque domain, employing diffusion models to generate high-dimensional force distributions to cope with complex contact dynamics. In terms of evolution, early work largely relied on rigid contact models or shallow force control. Recent studies, such as TacDiffusion, were the first to introduce diffusion strategies into the force domain, using generative models to process multimodal tactile feedback. This achieved an end-to-end mapping from motion commands to torque distributions, significantly improving zero-shot transfer capabilities and contact stability, marking a paradigm shift in assembly control from "position following" to "force field planning."

基础模型与数据蒸馏Foundation Models and Data Distillation

在机器人装配领域，基础模型与数据蒸馏正致力于解决人类演示数据稀缺、噪声大及触觉感知缺失的痛点。核心思路是通过强化学习生成高保真、无噪声的轨迹数据来蒸馏通用策略，或利用数据压缩技术将高维触觉时序信息注入视觉语言动作（VLA）模型。演进路径从单纯依赖人类演示转向“RL 合成数据 + 多模态融合”，旨在突破仿真到真实的鸿沟，赋予模型在接触丰富场景下的精准操作与泛化能力，推动装配任务从特定场景向通用自动化迈进。In the field of robotic assembly, foundation models and data distillation aim to address the pain points of scarce, noisy human demonstration data and the lack of tactile perception. The core approach involves using reinforcement learning to generate high-fidelity, noise-free trajectory data to distill general strategies, or utilizing data compression techniques to inject high-dimensional tactile temporal information into Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. The evolution path has shifted from solely relying on human demonstrations to "RL synthetic data + multimodal fusion," aiming to bridge the sim-to-real gap and endow models with precise manipulation and generalization capabilities in contact-rich scenarios, thereby advancing assembly tasks from specific scenarios toward general automation.

基准测试与泛化评估Benchmark Testing and Generalization Evaluation

Robot assembly 领域的基准测试与泛化评估正从封闭仿真向物理真实环境演进。早期工作多依赖仿真器，难以复现物理不确定性。FMB 等近期研究通过引入 3D 打印零件与标准化 Franka 机械臂，构建了兼顾物理复杂性与任务功能性的基准。其核心思路在于平衡“泛化性”与“可复现性”，通过定义明确的组装任务梯度，量化评估模型在零件位姿、材质及装配顺序变化下的鲁棒性。这一演进标志着评估重心从单纯追求算法性能，转向验证机器人在真实物理约束下的通用组装能力，为长尾场景下的具身智能提供了可量化的标尺。Benchmark testing and generalization evaluation in the field of robot assembly are evolving from closed simulations to physical real-world environments. Early work largely relied on simulators, making it difficult to reproduce physical uncertainties. Recent studies like FMB have constructed benchmarks balancing physical complexity and task functionality by introducing 3D-printed parts and standardized Franka robotic arms. The core approach lies in balancing "generalization" and "reproducibility" by defining clear assembly task gradients to quantitatively evaluate model robustness under variations in part pose, material, and assembly sequence. This evolution marks a shift in evaluation focus from solely pursuing algorithmic performance to verifying robots' general assembly capabilities under real physical constraints, providing a quantifiable yardstick for embodied intelligence in long-tail scenarios.
