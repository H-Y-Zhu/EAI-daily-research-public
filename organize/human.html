🧭 Learning from Humans / HRILearning from Humans / HRI

· 发展脉络· Development

该方向致力于解决具身智能从人类示范中学习复杂技能时的“具身鸿沟”与数据稀缺问题，核心在于如何低成本、高保真地采集人类数据并将其泛化至不同形态的机器人。演进路径从早期的专用遥操作硬件（如力反馈手套）逐步转向基于视频的大规模无监督学习、通用遥操作接口（UMI）以及世界模型驱动的仿真到真机迁移。近期发展聚焦于利用大语言模型（VLA）和扩散策略实现零样本迁移，通过触觉、力觉等多模态融合提升机器人在接触丰富场景下的柔顺性与泛化能力。This direction aims to address the 'embodiment gap' and data scarcity issues when embodied AI learns complex skills from human demonstrations. The core lies in how to collect human data at low cost with high fidelity and generalize it to robots of different morphologies. The evolutionary path has shifted from early dedicated teleoperation hardware (e.g., haptic gloves) to large-scale unsupervised learning based on video, universal teleoperation interfaces (UMI), and simulation-to-reality transfer driven by world models. Recent developments focus on achieving zero-shot transfer using Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and diffusion policies, enhancing robot compliance and generalization in contact-rich scenarios through multi-modal fusion of tactile and force feedback.

低成本遥操作与数据采集系统Low-Cost Teleoperation and Data Acquisition Systems

本方向旨在通过消费级硬件（VR、GoPro、外骨骼）与简易机械结构，打破高成本遥操作与数据采集的壁垒。核心思路是从“专用昂贵设备”向“通用低成本接口”演进：早期工作（如Mobile ALOHA）通过轮式底盘与静态数据共训练降低移动操作门槛；中期（如Universal Manipulation Interface, DexCap）利用手持夹爪、单目视觉及便携动捕系统实现“野外”灵活采集，解决形态差异与遮挡问题；近期（如OmniH2O, EgoMimic, RynnWorld）则进一步融合大模型、第一人称视频流及数字孪生技术，试图用人类视频或生成式世界模型替代物理机器人采集，实现数据规模化与长尾场景覆盖。尽管在触觉反馈与物理泛化上仍有局限，但低成本闭环已使具身智能从富豪实验室走向大众验证。This direction aims to break the barriers of high-cost teleoperation and data acquisition through consumer-grade hardware (VR, GoPro, exoskeletons) and simple mechanical structures. The core idea evolves from "dedicated expensive equipment" to "general low-cost interfaces": early work (e.g., Mobile ALOHA) lowered the threshold for mobile manipulation by co-training wheeled chassis with static data; mid-term efforts (e.g., Universal Manipulation Interface, DexCap) utilized handheld grippers, monocular vision, and portable motion capture systems to achieve flexible "field" data collection, addressing morphological differences and occlusion; recent work (e.g., OmniH2O, EgoMimic, RynnWorld) further integrates large models, first-person video streams, and digital twin technologies, attempting to replace physical robot data collection with human videos or generative world models to achieve data scaling and long-tail scenario coverage. Despite limitations in tactile feedback and physical generalization, the low-cost closed loop has propelled embodied intelligence from wealthy laboratories to public validation.
