该方向聚焦于具身智能（Embodied AI）从理论架构向安全、高效、可验证的实体系统演进，核心解决机器人在复杂物理环境中感知、决策与执行的可靠性问题。发展脉络从早期的单智能体控制与基础模型构建，迅速转向多智能体协作、世界模型的可解释性、以及针对物理世界的鲁棒性防御机制。近期研究重点在于构建硬件感知的安全内核、边缘计算下的实时推理、以及通过形式化验证解决具身智能的“对齐”与“攻击面”问题，标志着该领域正从实验性探索迈向工业级部署。This direction focuses on the evolution of Embodied AI from theoretical architectures to safe, efficient, and verifiable physical systems, corely addressing the reliability of perception, decision-making, and execution for robots in complex physical environments. The development trajectory has rapidly shifted from early single-agent control and foundational model construction to multi-agent collaboration, interpretability of world models, and robust defense mechanisms against the physical world. Recent research priorities center on building hardware-aware secure kernels, real-time inference under edge computing, and resolving the 'alignment' and 'attack surface' issues of embodied AI through formal verification, marking a transition of the field from experimental exploration to industrial-grade deployment.

具身智能安全与可信执行Embodied AI Security and Trusted Execution

具身智能安全与可信执行聚焦于解决传统软件层防御（如提示词工程）易被绕过、模型黑盒不可控及物理世界对抗风险高的问题。核心思路是从“软约束”转向“硬隔离”，通过硬件级不可篡改内核、FPGA 实时协调机制及推理时机制引导防御，构建物理与逻辑的双重防线。演进路径正从单纯依赖算法鲁棒性，转向构建包含机器验证内核、硬件执行边界及可解释推理监控的纵深防御体系，旨在确保智能体在动态物理环境中的绝对安全与可信。Embodied AI Security and Trusted Execution focuses on addressing the issues where traditional software-layer defenses (such as prompt engineering) are easily bypassed, model black boxes are uncontrollable, and physical-world adversarial risks are high. The core approach shifts from 'soft constraints' to 'hard isolation', constructing dual physical and logical defense lines through hardware-level tamper-proof kernels, FPGA real-time coordination mechanisms, and runtime guidance defense. The evolution path is moving from solely relying on algorithmic robustness to building a depth defense system encompassing machine-verified kernels, hardware execution boundaries, and explainable inference monitoring, aiming to ensure absolute safety and trustworthiness of agents in dynamic physical environments.
