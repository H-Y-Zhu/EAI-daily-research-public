On Global Regulatability of Robot Manipulators by Classical PID

Cheng Zhao, Jingru Zhu, Lei Guo
abspdf

论文点评Review

PID 控制堪称工业界的“常青树”，结构简单、成本低廉，尽管现代控制理论飞速发展，它依然是基石 (Aström & Hägglund, 2001)。然而，其理论根基是否牢固？本文给出了一个令人意外的答案：PID 的全局调节能力高度依赖自由度维度。对于单自由度机械臂，经典 PID 稳如泰山，作者给出了显式的增益设计条件；但对于多自由度 (N≥2) 系统，经典标量 PID 彻底失效，无论参数如何调整都无法实现全局渐近调节。这不仅是控制理论的胜利，更是对工程界“调参万能论”的辛辣嘲讽。虽然 PID 在工业现场依然大行其道，但本文揭示了其在高维非线性系统中的内在局限性。结果既肯定了 PID 在简单场景下的有效性，也划定了其理论能力的边界，提醒研究者不要盲目迷信经典结构的普适性。
PID control is regarded as the "evergreen tree" of the industrial world, featuring a simple structure and low cost. Despite the rapid development of modern control theory, it remains a cornerstone (Aström & Hägglund, 2001). However, is its theoretical foundation solid? This paper provides a surprising answer: the global regulation capability of PID highly depends on the dimension of degrees of freedom. For single-degree-of-freedom robotic arms, classical PID is rock-solid, and the authors provide explicit gain design conditions; however, for multi-degree-of-freedom (N≥2) systems, classical scalar PID fails completely, and global asymptotic regulation cannot be achieved regardless of parameter adjustments. This is not only a victory for control theory but also a biting satire on the engineering world's "parameter tuning omnipotence theory". Although PID is still prevalent in industrial settings, this paper reveals its inherent limitations in high-dimensional nonlinear systems. The results both affirm the effectiveness of PID in simple scenarios and delineate the boundaries of its theoretical capabilities, reminding researchers not to blindly worship the universality of classical structures.

研究动机Motivation

经典 PID 控制因结构简单、成本低廉，在工业控制中占据统治地位 (Aström & Hägglund, 2001)。然而，对于非线性机器人系统，其全局稳定性长期是开放问题。PD 控制已被证明可全局稳定无摩擦机械臂 (Takegaki & Arimoto, 1981)，但引入积分项消除稳态误差后，稳定性分析变得极具挑战。Kelly (1998) 指出，尽管 PID 广泛应用，但缺乏完整的 PID 控制机械臂全局稳定性证明。现有研究多通过修改控制器结构（如非线性积分、饱和机制）来恢复全局调节 (Ortega et al., 1995; Santibáñez et al., 2008)，但这回避了核心问题：经典标量 PID 本身是否具备此能力？此外，虽然针对不确定非线性系统的 PID 框架已建立 (Zhao & Guo, 2017a)，但往往忽略物理结构。本文旨在填补这一理论空白，明确经典 PID 的能力边界，区分维度依赖的稳定性特征，为系统设计与参数选择提供严格理论依据。
Classical PID control occupies a dominant position in industrial control due to its simple structure and low cost (Aström & Hägglund, 2001). However, for nonlinear robotic systems, its global stability has long been an open problem. PD control has been proven to globally stabilize frictionless robotic arms (Takegaki & Arimoto, 1981), but after introducing the integral term to eliminate steady-state error, stability analysis becomes extremely challenging. Kelly (1998) points out that although PID is widely used, there is a lack of complete proof of global stability for PID-controlled robotic arms. Existing research mostly restores global regulation by modifying controller structures (such as nonlinear integration, saturation mechanisms) (Ortega et al., 1995; Santibáñez et al., 2008), but this avoids the core question: does classical scalar PID itself possess this capability? Furthermore, although a PID framework for uncertain nonlinear systems has been established (Zhao & Guo, 2017a), it often ignores physical structure. This paper aims to fill this theoretical gap, clarify the capability boundaries of classical PID, distinguish dimension-dependent stability characteristics, and provide a rigorous theoretical basis for system design and parameter selection.

方法Method

论文采用理论证明与反例构造相结合的方法。首先针对单自由度 (1-DOF) 情况，构建基于能量的 Lyapunov 函数，结合欧拉 - 拉格朗日动力学结构，推导出了 PID 增益的显式定量设计条件，证明了全局指数稳定性。证明中引入了增广坐标变换，将系统转化为特定形式，利用惯性矩阵有界性和重力项 Lipschitz 性质，构造了正定且径向无界的 Lyapunov 候选函数。其次，针对多自由度 (N≥2) 情况，构造了一个满足标准结构假设的刚性机械臂反例。通过详细分析闭环系统的解的存在性与收敛性，证明了对于任意标量 PID 增益组合，均存在初始条件或期望配置使得调节目标失败。证明过程分为三种增益情形讨论：积分与微分增益同号、积分增益为零、以及混合情况。利用线性化分析和轨迹追踪，揭示了多维空间中 PID 控制无法克服的内在局限性，而非解的有限逃逸问题。
The paper adopts a method combining theoretical proof and counterexample construction. First, for the single-degree-of-freedom (1-DOF) case, an energy-based Lyapunov function is constructed, combined with Euler-Lagrange dynamic structure, to derive explicit quantitative design conditions for PID gains, proving global exponential stability. The proof introduces an augmented coordinate transformation to convert the system into a specific form, utilizing the boundedness of the inertia matrix and the Lipschitz property of the gravity term to construct a positive definite and radially unbounded Lyapunov candidate function. Second, for the multi-degree-of-freedom (N≥2) case, a rigid robotic arm counterexample satisfying standard structural assumptions is constructed. By detailed analysis of the existence and convergence of solutions for the closed-loop system, it is proven that for any scalar PID gain combination, there exist initial conditions or desired configurations such that the regulation target fails. The proof process is divided into three gain scenarios: integral and derivative gains having the same sign, integral gain being zero, and mixed cases. Utilizing linearization analysis and trajectory tracking, it reveals the inherent limitations that PID control cannot overcome in multi-dimensional space, rather than finite escape time problems of solutions.

实验Experiments

本文属于纯理论分析，未提供数值仿真或硬件实验数据。所谓的“实验”体现为严格的数学证明与反例构造。在 1-DOF 部分，通过 Lyapunov 稳定性理论验证了增益条件的有效性，证明了误差与速度指数收敛。在 N-DOF 部分，构造的具体机械臂模型（包含特定的惯性矩阵、科里奥利力及重力项）作为反例，验证了 PID 失效的必然性。结果显示，对于构造的系统，闭环解虽全局存在，但误差并不收敛至零，甚至存在周期解。这一发现表明，多自由度机械臂的 PID 全局调节问题并非技术难题，而是理论上的不可能性。这为后续研究指明了方向：若要实现多维全局调节，需引入矩阵增益、模型依赖补偿或修改积分结构。
This paper belongs to pure theoretical analysis and does not provide numerical simulation or hardware experimental data. The so-called "experiments" are embodied in rigorous mathematical proofs and counterexample construction. In the 1-DOF part, the effectiveness of gain conditions is verified through Lyapunov stability theory, proving exponential convergence of error and velocity. In the N-DOF part, the constructed specific robotic arm model (containing specific inertia matrix, Coriolis force, and gravity terms) serves as a counterexample, verifying the inevitability of PID failure. Results show that for the constructed system, although closed-loop solutions exist globally, the error does not converge to zero, and even periodic solutions exist. This finding indicates that the PID global regulation problem for multi-degree-of-freedom robotic arms is not a technical difficulty, but a theoretical impossibility. This points the direction for subsequent research: to achieve multi-dimensional global regulation, matrix gains, model-dependent compensation, or modified integral structures need to be introduced.
