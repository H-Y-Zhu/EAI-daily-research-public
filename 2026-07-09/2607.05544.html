Dynamic Evaluation of Classical and Control-Aware Optimal Trajectory Planning in Robot Manipulators

Bhanuka Dayawansa, Rohan Munasinghe
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文最有趣的地方在于它戳破了“运动学平滑等于执行高效”的泡沫。传统规划器（三次、五次、梯形）只管关节空间轨迹好看，不管动力学和力矩代价，导致实际跑起来 PID 控制器累得半死，产生不必要的校正动作。作者没发明新算法，而是搞了个“公平竞技场”，把经典方法和控制感知规划器放在完全相同的非线性动力学和 PID 控制器下跑。结果很打脸：高阶平滑没带来动力学效率。虽然只是离线生成，但胜在控制意识强，力矩和成本降得漂亮。不过，中点线性化在超大运动下可能仍有误差，且未涉及真实机器人验证，略显遗憾，但作为基准研究已足够犀利。
The most interesting aspect of this paper is that it bursts the bubble that "kinematic smoothness equals execution efficiency." Traditional planners (cubic, quintic, trapezoidal) only care about joint space trajectories looking good, ignoring dynamics and torque costs, causing the PID controller to work excessively hard during actual execution, generating unnecessary corrective actions. The authors did not invent a new algorithm, but created a "fair arena," running classic methods and control-aware planners under exactly the same nonlinear dynamics and PID controller. The results were quite striking: high-order smoothness did not yield dynamic efficiency. Although it is generated offline, it excels in control awareness, achieving impressive reductions in torque and cost. However, midpoint linearization may still incur errors under extremely large motions, and real robot validation is not included, which is somewhat regrettable, but as a benchmark study, it is sufficiently incisive.

研究动机Motivation

机器人轨迹规划长期存在“运动学至上”的误区。经典方法如三次、五次多项式和梯形速度曲线，因简单平滑被广泛使用 [1]，但本质是运动学的，忽略了动力学和控制努力 [2]。这导致名义上平滑的轨迹在执行非线性动力学时，可能产生不利的加速度分布和过大的校正力矩 [3]。现有对比研究常因控制器、仿真环境或动态模型不同，难以隔离规划器本身的影响 [12], [13]。因此，亟需一个统一框架，在相同闭环非线性执行条件下，公平评估轨迹生成对执行行为的影响，验证高阶运动学平滑是否真能对应动力学高效执行，填补了现有文献在公平对比方面的空白。
Robot trajectory planning has long suffered from the misconception of "kinematics supremacy." Classic methods such as cubic, quintic polynomials, and trapezoidal velocity profiles are widely used [1] due to simple smoothness, but are essentially kinematic, ignoring dynamics and control effort [2]. This leads to nominally smooth trajectories potentially producing unfavorable acceleration distributions and excessive corrective torques when executing nonlinear dynamics [3]. Existing comparative studies often struggle to isolate the influence of the planner itself due to differences in controllers, simulation environments, or dynamic models [12], [13]. Therefore, a unified framework is urgently needed to fairly evaluate the impact of trajectory generation on execution behavior under identical closed-loop nonlinear execution conditions, verifying whether high-order kinematic smoothness truly corresponds to dynamic efficient execution, filling the gap in fair comparison in existing literature.

方法Method

整体流程是离线生成轨迹，闭环执行评估。核心框架为有限时域最优控制。首先，针对大点间运动，引入中点线性化策略近似非线性动力学，通过偏差变量将非线性方程线性化，构建离散时间预测模型 [Eq 7-11]。其次，构建代价函数，同时惩罚状态偏差和控制量，确保轨迹既准确又省力，并施加终端约束 [Eq 18]。关键创新在于统一评估框架：所有规划器（包括经典基线）均使用相同的非线性 UR5 模型、相同的前馈辅助 PID 控制器、相同的增益和力矩约束 [Section V]。这使得执行差异可直接归因于轨迹生成方法。最后，通过求解二次规划获得最优控制序列，作为离线轨迹生成器，而非实时 MPC 求解器，平衡了计算复杂度与性能。
The overall process is offline trajectory generation, closed-loop execution evaluation. The core framework is finite horizon optimal control. First, for large point-to-point motions, a midpoint linearization strategy is introduced to approximate nonlinear dynamics, linearizing nonlinear equations through deviation variables to construct a discrete-time prediction model [Eq 7-11]. Second, a cost function is constructed, penalizing both state deviation and control effort, ensuring trajectories are both accurate and energy-saving, and applying terminal constraints [Eq 18]. The key innovation lies in the unified evaluation framework: all planners (including classic baselines) use the same nonlinear UR5 model, the same feedforward-auxiliary PID controller, and the same gains and torque constraints [Section V]. This allows execution differences to be directly attributed to the trajectory generation method. Finally, the optimal control sequence is obtained by solving quadratic programming, serving as an offline trajectory generator rather than a real-time MPC solver, balancing computational complexity and performance.

实验Experiments

实验基于 3-DoF 非线性 UR5 机械臂仿真，任务为固定时长的点对点关节空间运动。基线包括三次、五次多项式和梯形速度规划。评测指标涵盖 RMS 跟踪误差、RMS 校正力矩、累积校正控制活动及闭环执行成本。结果显示，控制感知规划器在所有指标上均优于经典方法。RMS 跟踪误差最低，校正力矩减少 28-41%，执行成本减少 48-62%。这证实了运动学平滑不足以保证动力学效率。消融分析虽未明确列出，但通过对比不同规划器在统一框架下的表现，有效隔离了规划器影响，验证了控制感知方法的有效性，为工业场景提供了更优的离线规划选择。
Experiments are based on 3-DoF nonlinear UR5 manipulator simulation, with tasks being fixed-duration point-to-point joint space motions. Baselines include cubic, quintic polynomials, and trapezoidal velocity planning. Evaluation metrics cover RMS tracking error, RMS corrective torque, cumulative corrective control activity, and closed-loop execution cost. Results show that the control-aware planner outperforms classic methods on all metrics. RMS tracking error is lowest, corrective torque reduced by 28-41%, execution cost reduced by 48-62%. This confirms that kinematic smoothness is insufficient to guarantee dynamic efficiency. Although ablation analysis is not explicitly listed, by comparing the performance of different planners under the unified framework, the planner influence is effectively isolated, validating the effectiveness of the control-aware method, providing a better offline planning choice for industrial scenarios.
