Insert Anything: Image Insertion via In-Context Editing in DiT

Wensong Song, Hongbo Jiang, Zongxin Yang, Ruijie Quan, Yi Yang
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文试图用 DiT 解决图像插入的“大一统”问题，听起来很美好，毕竟现在模型都卷 Transformer。作者搞了个 AnyInsertion 数据集，号称 120K 对，覆盖了人、物、衣服，这点比那些只盯着特定任务的 [3][6] 强多了。不过，所谓的“统一”是不是只是把不同任务堆在一个模型里？In-Context Editing 这个概念被炒得很热，这里用 Diptych/Triptych 强行把参考图塞进上下文，算是个聪明的 trick，但真的比 ControlNet 这种外挂网络更高效吗？实验结果确实刷榜了，PSNR 和 FID 都好看，但推理成本呢？毕竟 DiT 比 UNet 重。总的来说，是个不错的工程整合，但“通用”的帽子戴得有点大，毕竟还是依赖特定数据集训练，离真正的 zero-shot 还有距离。
This paper attempts to use DiT to address the "unification" problem of image insertion, which sounds appealing, given that models are currently racing with Transformers. The authors developed an AnyInsertion dataset, claiming 120K pairs, covering people, objects, and clothes, which is much superior to those focusing solely on specific tasks [3][6]. However, is the so-called "unification" merely stacking different tasks into a single model? The concept of In-Context Editing has been heavily hyped; here Diptych/Triptych are used to forcibly insert reference images into the context, counting as a clever trick, but is it truly more efficient than external networks like ControlNet? The experimental results indeed topped the leaderboard, with PSNR and FID looking favorable, but what about inference cost? After all, DiT is heavier than UNet. Overall, it is a decent engineering integration, but the "generalization" label is worn a bit too large, after all, it still relies on training with specific datasets, still a distance away from true zero-shot.

研究动机Motivation

当前图像插入任务面临三大痛点。首先是任务特异性太强，像 AnyDoor [3] 或 MimicBrush [2] 往往只针对特定对象（如人或物体），缺乏通用性。其次是控制模式僵化，要么只能靠 Mask [2, 3, 6]，要么只能靠 Text [11]，用户无法灵活切换。最后是视觉一致性难保证，插入的元素容易和背景风格冲突，或者丢失参考图的细节特征 [11, 25]。现有的参考生成方法如 IP-Adapter [48] 或 DreamBooth [34] 虽然能保留主体，但缺乏对目标场景的可控插入能力。因此，作者提出需要一个能同时支持 Mask 和 Text 引导，且能保持身份特征与场景和谐统一的框架。
Current image insertion tasks face three major pain points. First, task specificity is too strong; models like AnyDoor [3] or MimicBrush [2] often target only specific objects (e.g., humans or objects), lacking universality. Second, control modes are rigid; either relying solely on Mask [2, 3, 6] or Text [11], users cannot switch flexibly. Finally, visual consistency is difficult to guarantee; inserted elements easily conflict with background styles or lose detail features of the reference image [11, 25]. Existing reference generation methods such as IP-Adapter [48] or DreamBooth [34] can preserve the subject, but lack controllable insertion capability for target scenes. Therefore, the authors propose a framework that can simultaneously support Mask and Text guidance, and maintain identity features and scene harmony and unity.

方法Method

整体框架基于 DiT 架构（FLUX.1 Fill 改进），核心是 In-Context Editing。Pipeline 分为数据准备、编码与注意力融合、生成三个阶段。
1. 数据构建：AnyInsertion 数据集包含 159K 样本，分为 Mask-prompt 和 Text-prompt 两类，覆盖人、物、服装等。
2. Polyptych 输入：针对 Mask 引导设计 Diptych（参考图 + 掩码源图），针对 Text 引导设计 Triptych（参考图 + 源图 + 生成区），将参考图作为上下文直接拼接，而非单独编码。
3. 多模态注意力：利用 DiT 的 multimodal attention，图像分支处理拼接后的图像块和噪声，文本分支处理语义提示。通过 Q, K, V 的拼接实现视觉与文本的联合注意力 [31]。
4. 语义引导：使用 SigLIP 图像编码器提取参考图语义，辅助保留高频细节。
5. 训练：基于 Flow Matching 优化，冻结 VAE 编码器以保留细节，LoRA 微调 DiT 层。
The overall framework is based on the DiT architecture (FLUX.1 Fill improvement), with the core being In-Context Editing.
The pipeline is divided into three stages: data preparation, encoding and attention fusion, and generation.
1. Data Construction: The AnyInsertion dataset contains 159K samples, divided into Mask-prompt and Text-prompt categories, covering people, objects, clothing, etc.
2. Polyptych Input: Diptych (Reference Image + Mask Source Image) is designed for Mask guidance, Triptych (Reference Image + Source Image + Generation Area) is designed for Text guidance, concatenating the reference image directly as context rather than encoding it separately.
3. Multimodal Attention: Utilizing DiT's multimodal attention, the image branch processes concatenated image patches and noise, the text branch processes semantic prompts. Visual and textual joint attention is achieved through concatenation of Q, K, V [31].
4. Semantic Guidance: Using SigLIP image encoder to extract reference image semantics, assisting in preserving high-frequency details.
5. Training: Optimized based on Flow Matching, freezing VAE encoder to preserve details, LoRA fine-tuning DiT layers.

实验Experiments

在 AnyInsertion、DreamBooth 和 VTON-HD 三个基准上评估。对比基线包括 AnyDoor [3], MimicBrush [2], ACE++ [25], CatVTON [6] 等。指标涵盖 PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, FID。
结果方面，在对象插入任务中，Mask 引导下 SSIM 提升至 0.8791，显著优于 AnyDoor 的 0.7648 [Table 2]。Text 引导下 LPIPS 降至 0.2011，优于 AnyEdit [49] [Table 3]。服装插入在 VTON-HD 上 FID 达 19.51，优于 CatVTON 的 24.80 [Table 4]。人像插入同样取得 SOTA [Table 5]。消融实验表明，移除 In-Context 编辑或语义引导会导致细节丢失 [Fig 6, Table 6]。证明多模态上下文和特定数据集训练对性能至关重要。
Evaluated on three benchmarks: AnyInsertion, DreamBooth, and VTON-HD. Comparison baselines include AnyDoor [3], MimicBrush [2], ACE++ [25], CatVTON [6], etc. Metrics cover PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, FID.
In terms of results, in object insertion tasks, SSIM under Mask guidance improved to 0.8791, significantly better than AnyDoor's 0.7648 [Table 2]. Under Text guidance, LPIPS dropped to 0.2011, better than AnyEdit [49] [Table 3]. Clothing insertion achieved FID of 19.51 on VTON-HD, better than CatVTON's 24.80 [Table 4]. Portrait insertion also achieved SOTA [Table 5]. Ablation studies show that removing In-Context editing or semantic guidance leads to detail loss [Fig 6, Table 6]. Proving that multimodal context and specific dataset training are crucial for performance.
