Sensing Which Modality Matters: Evidence-Gated Regularization for Robust VLA Policies

Yue Yang, Diego Romeres, Chiori Hori, Gedas Bertasius, Daniel Szafir, Siddarth Jain
abspdf

论文点评Review

这篇论文给那些盲目相信“多模态融合即正义”的 VLA 研究者泼了一盆冷水。作者犀利地指出，在有限的机器人演示数据下，模型往往不是在学习任务，而是在搞“联觉诈骗”——把无关的传感器信号（比如地板纹理）和任务强行绑定，导致一旦这些“背景板”被遮挡或干扰，机器人就当场宕机。提出的 EGR 方法确实有点东西，它不依赖复杂的架构修改，而是用一种“证据门控”机制，教模型在训练时学会“看人下菜碟”：没用的传感器直接无视，有用的传感器单挑全场。不过，这招的局限性也很明显：证据计算严重依赖预定义的规则（如分割掩码或接触特征），如果任务本身没有明确的“有用信号”定义（比如复杂的物体重定向），这套机制就瞎了。此外，虽然推理零开销，但训练时的证据计算和采样策略增加了工程复杂度，对于追求端到端黑盒的社区来说，这种“白盒式”的干预可能显得过于繁琐。
This paper pours cold water on VLA researchers who blindly believe that "multimodal fusion is justice". The authors sharply point out that under limited robot demonstration data, models are often not learning tasks, but engaging in "synesthesia fraud"—forcibly binding irrelevant sensor signals (such as floor textures) to tasks, causing the robot to crash on the spot once these "backdrops" are occluded or interfered with. The proposed EGR method is indeed effective; it does not rely on complex architecture modifications, but uses an "evidence gating" mechanism to teach the model to "discriminate based on utility" during training: ignore useless sensors outright, let useful sensors handle the task single-handedly. However, the limitations of this approach are also obvious: evidence calculation relies heavily on predefined rules (such as segmentation masks or contact features); if the task itself lacks a clear definition of "useful signals" (such as complex object reorientation), this mechanism becomes blind. Furthermore, although inference has zero overhead, the evidence calculation and sampling strategies during training increase engineering complexity; for the community pursuing end-to-end black boxes, this "white-box" intervention may appear too cumbersome.

研究动机Motivation

研究旨在解决 VLA 策略中普遍存在的“模态纠缠”（modality entanglement）问题。现有 VLA 模型通常采用早期融合策略，由于机器人演示数据规模小且同质化严重，模型容易习得虚假的跨模态相关性（shortcut learning）[8, 9]。具体表现为两种失败模式：一是“干扰敏感性”（nuisance sensitivity），即模型对当前状态无关的传感器（如只看地板的手腕相机）过度依赖，一旦这些传感器被物理遮挡或干扰，策略即失效；二是“单模态不足性”（single-modality insufficiency），即当多个传感器同时提供信息时，若仅剩一个有效传感器，模型无法仅凭该传感器完成任务。现有方法如随机模态丢弃（ModDrop）[12] 或对抗一致性训练 [13] 仅处理通用的传感器缺失，无法根据状态动态选择模态；而基于注意力的方法 [28] 或显式门控（如 TacVLA）[15] 要么继承了数据偏差，要么仅针对特定模态（如触觉接触），缺乏通用的、基于任务结构的模态选择机制。
The research aims to solve the prevalent "modality entanglement" problem in VLA policies. Existing VLA models typically adopt early fusion strategies; due to the small scale and high homogeneity of robot demonstration data, models easily learn spurious cross-modal correlations (shortcut learning) [8, 9]. Specifically manifested in two failure modes: first is "nuisance sensitivity", where the model overly relies on sensors irrelevant to the current state (such as wrist cameras only looking at the floor); once these sensors are physically occluded or interfered with, the policy fails; second is "single-modality insufficiency", meaning when multiple sensors provide information simultaneously, if only one effective sensor remains, the model cannot complete the task relying solely on that sensor. Existing methods such as random modality dropout (ModDrop) [12] or adversarial consistency training [13] only handle generic sensor missingness and cannot dynamically select modalities based on state; while attention-based methods [28] or explicit gating (such as TacVLA) [15] either inherit data biases or only target specific modalities (such as tactile contact), lacking a general, task-structure-based modality selection mechanism.

方法Method

EGR 的核心思想是将“模态相关性评估”与“策略正则化”解耦，分为证据计算和门控正则化两个阶段。首先，针对每个帧和每个传感器计算“证据分数”（Evidence Score），作为任务相关性的启发式指标。对于视觉模态，证据基于任务相关物体（焦点物体和交互物体）在图像中的可见面积，并引入交互门控防止背景物体干扰；对于触觉模态，证据基于接触信号或触觉特征相似度。其次，利用证据分数通过铰链权重（hinge weights）门控两个一致性目标：当证据低时，激活“不变性损失”（Linv），强制模型在无关传感器被破坏时动作不变；当证据高时，激活“充分性损失”（Lsuff），强制模型在仅保留高证据传感器时动作与全模态一致。训练时采用重要性采样（Importance Sampling）技术，仅对高权重模态进行扰动计算，将推理开销从 O(|M|) 降至常数。最终损失函数为流匹配模仿损失与两个正则化项的加权和。该方法无需修改基座架构，且仅在训练阶段引入计算，推理时零开销。
The core idea of EGR is to decouple "modality relevance assessment" from "policy regularization", divided into two stages: evidence calculation and gating regularization. First, calculate an "Evidence Score" for each frame and each sensor as a heuristic metric for task relevance. For the visual modality, evidence is based on the visible area of task-relevant objects (focus objects and interaction objects) in the image, and interaction gating is introduced to prevent background object interference; for the tactile modality, evidence is based on contact signals or tactile feature similarity. Second, use the evidence scores to gate two consistency targets via hinge weights: when evidence is low, activate "invariance loss" (Linv), forcing the model to keep actions unchanged when irrelevant sensors are destroyed; when evidence is high, activate "sufficiency loss" (Lsuff), forcing the model to keep actions consistent with full modalities when only high-evidence sensors are retained. During training, Importance Sampling technology is used, performing perturbation calculations only on high-weight modalities, reducing inference overhead from O(|M|) to constant. The final loss function is a weighted sum of flow matching imitation loss and the two regularization terms. This method requires no modification to the base architecture and introduces computation only during the training phase, with zero overhead during inference.

实验Experiments

作者在 BEHAVIOR-1K 基准上构建了包含 47 个技能段的评测集，并部署于双机械臂（Kinova）和单臂触觉（MELFA+GelSight）两套真实机器人平台。实验对比了 vanilla π0.5 [7] 和 ModDrop [12] 基线。在仿真中，EGR 在完整模态下成功率提升 31%，在无关传感器干扰下提升 75%，在单模态回退下提升 120%。在真实机器人实验中，面对物理干扰物，双机械臂成功率从 30% 飙升至 85%（+183%），触觉平台从 55% 提升至 70%（+27%）。消融实验表明，EGR 不仅提升了鲁棒性，甚至在干净输入下也略微提升了性能，证明其有助于模型学习更本质的任务特征。
The authors constructed an evaluation set containing 47 skill segments on the BEHAVIOR-1K benchmark and deployed it on two real robot platforms: dual-arm (Kinova) and single-arm tactile (MELFA+GelSight). Experiments compared vanilla π0.5 [7] and ModDrop [12] baselines. In simulation, EGR improved success rate by 31% under full modalities, by 75% under irrelevant sensor interference, and by 120% under single-modality fallback. In real robot experiments, facing physical interference objects, the dual-arm success rate surged from 30% to 85% (+183%), and the tactile platform improved from 55% to 70% (+27%). Ablation studies show that EGR not only improved robustness but also slightly improved performance even on clean inputs, proving it helps the model learn more essential task features.
